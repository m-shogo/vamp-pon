Use before creating or reviewing:

- avatar / player-side designs
- monsters
- items
- backgrounds
- UI screens
- icons
- cards
- graphic frames
- art direction boards

## Core rule

Every visual task must pass:

1. art direction fit
2. design-role fit
3. visual identity locks
4. pixel-art craft check
5. gameplay readability check
6. 80-point rubric
7. production promotion gate when relevant

## Quality target

Do not accept 60-point work.
Target 80-point app quality before production promotion.

## Continuous brush-up

Do not stop at one pass.
Use:

```txt
brief -> A/B/C prototype -> review -> merge best parts -> v2 -> review -> v3 -> promotion check
```

Under-80 work must stay prototype or iterate.

## Do not

- Do not rely on one-shot AI output.
- Do not use hair or color only for identity.
- Do not make black blob enemies.
- Do not make beautiful but noisy backgrounds.
- Do not make decorative UI that reduces readability.
- Do not promote without review evidence.
