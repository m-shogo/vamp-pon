# Pixel Art Pitfalls

This guide lists weak patterns to avoid when making Vamp Pon player sprites.

## Mushroom hood

Problem:
- The hood dominates the face and body.
- Legs and costume are unreadable.
- The character reads as a blob instead of a person.

Fix:
- Keep hood height around 40-45% of the sprite.
- Keep face height around 20-25%.
- Preserve costume hem, legs, and small shoes.
- Place the lantern as a clear side silhouette.

## Script-shaped art

Problem:
- The sprite looks like clean geometry, not crafted pixel art.
- Shadows look mechanical.
- Hood and face look like simple ovals.

Fix:
- Add small hand-made asymmetry.
- Use color clusters, not bands.
- Finish the face, bangs, costume edge, and lantern at pixel level.

## Dot-only eyes

Problem:
- The face has no charm.
- The character is readable but not memorable.

Fix:
- Add a tiny catchlight.
- Keep the mouth small.
- Add subtle cheeks.

## Weak lantern

Problem:
- The lantern looks like a yellow dot.
- It is not connected to the hand.
- It can be confused with gameplay markers.

Fix:
- Show handle, cage, warm core, and restrained glow.
- Keep it attached to the right hand.
- Keep glow weaker than gameplay hit indicators.

## Triangle dress

Problem:
- The body reads as a placeholder triangle.
- Legs disappear.

Fix:
- Add old-paper cream body volume.
- Add a small hem accent.
- Keep tiny shoes visible.

## Pixel noise

Problem:
- Too many isolated pixels make the sprite muddy.

Fix:
- Use readable color clusters.
- Avoid dithering on the face.
- Use texture mostly for backgrounds or larger surfaces.

## Heavy outline

Problem:
- The sprite becomes too hard and loses the soft picture-book feel.

Fix:
- Use dark purple or navy instead of pure black.
- Weaken outline on lit sides.
- Avoid outlining every edge with the same strength.

## Reporting pitfall

Problem:
- The report sounds good, but the before/after image does not show a real improvement.

Fix:
- Require before/after review.
- Keep weak work as draft.
- Do not promote assets below the quality gate.
