# 1. S2 philosophical difference from Sakuyaza

## S1 / Sakuyaza

Core danger:

> 本人のために、正しいname / record / meaning / routeを先に固定する。

Recognition / Record / Belonging。

## S2 / new team

Core danger:

> **全部は守れない。だから、配る順番・直す順番・残す道・休ませる人・使う資源を決めなければならない。**

This is not automatically wrong.

Reality often requires:

- triage
- budgets
- resource allocation
- maintenance priority
- public transport planning
- environmental limits
- care schedules
- labor schedules
- reconstruction order

S2 tragedy begins when:

```txt
necessary prioritization
↓
priority becomes permanent status
↓
low priority becomes low worth
↓
not chosen becomes disposable
```

S2 counter-thesis Candidate:

> **全部を同時には守れない。それでも「今回は選ばれなかった」を「この人は選ばなくていい」に変えない。**

This differs from S1's choice/identity thesis while sharing the series-wide Third Option principle.

# 2. Team naming — OPEN

## Preferred working candidate

# **衡守会（こうしゅかい）**

Status: `PREFERRED_WORKING_CANDIDATE / NOT CANON`

Working reading:

- `衡` = balance / weighing relation
- `守` = protect / maintain
- `会` = a gathered association, intentionally different from S1 `座`

Story reading:

> **「崩れないよう、釣り合いを守る者たち」**

The name sounds protective before it sounds threatening, which suits S2.

Important:

- no fixed religious meaning is intended
- no direct real institution is intended
- trademark / final commercial clearance is separate later review

## Alternative candidate A

**配律会（はいりつかい）**

Meaning direction:

> 配ることにrule / rhythmを与える。

Strength:

- institutional / allocation tone

Risk:

- slightly too abstract / artificial

## Alternative candidate B

**余火連（よかれん）**

Meaning direction:

> 残った火を誰へ渡すか。

Strength:

- poetic, survival / inheritance feel

Risk:

- fire imagery may become too close to series lamp identity
- S2 is broader than scarcity/fire

## Rejected working candidate

**分灯会**

Reason:

- `分灯` is already used in real religious / memorial contexts for sharing a flame/light.
- avoid unnecessary association when a fresh fictional brand is available.

Research notes:

- https://kotobank.jp/word/%E7%A6%8F%E7%94%B0%E6%B5%B7-1200918
- https://da.lib.kobe-u.ac.jp/da/eqb/0100035799/

No final team name is locked by this file.

# 4. Working roster — 8 NEW enemy characters

All eight are **new S2 antagonist candidates**.
They are not Future15, not Current21, not Sakuyaza renamed.

## 4.1 イサナ — Big-picture planner

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **圧倒的な大局観 / 本当に街を良くできる有能さ / local lifeを数字へ縮める怖さ**

### Core belief

> 「全体が流れれば、救える人数は増える。」

### Fear

> 一部の例外を守るために、街全体が止まること。

### Violation

> low-flow route / small district / hard-to-measure needを、未来のためのcostとして先に切る。

### Why players can like Isana

- genuinely sees connections others miss
- remembers transport / energy / housing dependencies
- does not enjoy sacrifice
- takes responsibility instead of hiding behind “someone decided”
- can admit a model was wrong, but is slow to admit a person cannot be modeled

### Ordinary habit

地図を見ると、まず目的地ではなく**流れが詰まる場所**へ目が行く。

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Michiru `一本線計画事件`

Secondary:

- Yui optimization consequence
- Asa public-resource side

### Avoid

- generic cold mastermind
- fixed leader just because charismatic
- “development = evil”

### Visual candidate

- tall / open posture
- layered city maps, not magic hologram by default
- practical long outerwear, visible worn fold lines
- broad gesture drawing routes in air / paper

---

## 4.2 カンナ — Repair triage specialist

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **頼れる修理屋 / 現場判断の速さ / 「直さない」と決める側の怖さ**

### Core belief

> 「全部を直そうとしたら、本当に直せるものまで壊れる。」

### Fear

> scarce time / parts / laborを救えない物へ使い、全体を共倒れにすること。

### Violation

> `repairable / beyond repair`の判断を、objectからperson / household / communityへ拡張する。

### Difference from Sakuyaza Tsuguri

**ツグリ:** 壊れているなら本人が望む前に全部直そうとする。

**カンナ:** 全部は直せないから、**何を直さないか**を先に選ぶ。

They are thematic opposites, not reskins.

### Ordinary habit

loose screw / frayed strapを見つけてもすぐ触らず、まず:

> 「今これ直す必要ある？」

と聞く。

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Tomori reconstruction / repair
- Asa body maintenance / repair-resource side

### Fan appeal

- dry humor
- skilled hands
- hates waste
- secretly keeps a box of “not worth repairing” small objects she could not throw away

### Avoid

- Tsuguri copy
- “mechanic = tomboy” shortcut
- inherited craft talent explanation

---

## 4.3 ナナセ — Access / route coordinator

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **道を必ず一本は残す安心感 / majority accessを守るためminor routeを諦める矛盾**

### Core belief

> 「全員に完璧な道は作れない。でも誰も動けないよりいい。」

### Fear

> too many alternatives make emergency movement collapse.

### Violation

> “most people can use it”をminimum accessの代わりにする。

### Difference from Sakuyaza Michigure

**ミチグレ:** wrong routeを消す。

**ナナセ:** limited budgetで**残すrouteを選ぶ**。

Nanase can preserve routes Michigure would erase, yet still exclude minority access.

### Ordinary habit

building / restaurantへ入る時、入口より先に**帰りのroute**を見る。

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Michiru `一本線計画事件`
- accessibility / transport / evacuation planning

Secondary:

- Amane series-reservoir interaction Candidate

### Avoid

- wheelchair/accessibility issue = one disabled person lesson
- route engineer as emotionless technocrat

## 4.4 ヨシノ — Resource / environment steward

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **未来世代を本気で守る穏やかさ / present peopleへ“今だけ我慢”を永久化する怖さ**

### Core belief

> 「使い切らないことも、誰かへ渡すことだ。」

### Fear

> short-term comfort consumes what future people need.

### Violation

> reserve / environmental protection / energy limitsを、current residentsの生活事情より上位のabsolute ruleへする。

### Ordinary habit

食事で最後に残った一口を捨てず、誰も要らなければ自分で食べる。

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Michiru energy / pollution / development

Secondary:

- Tomori scarcity echo
- future resource ethics

### Fan appeal

- calm gardener / caretaker energy
- knows seasons and maintenance
- can be the first S2 antagonist to openly say “we were wrong” about one local restriction while still defending the larger principle

### Avoid

- environmentalist caricature
- older = wise / conservative shortcut

## 4.5 ケイ — Care / response allocator

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **緊急時に頼れる冷静さ / probabilityがperson valueへ変わる瞬間の怖さ**

### Core belief

> 「今助ければ助かる人から助ける。それを決めない方が残酷だ。」

### Fear

> indecision uses limited care/time until everyone becomes harder to save.

### Violation

> `chance to benefit` / `time required` / `support cost`を、本人のworthへすり替える。

### Important ethical guard

This is fictional social design, not a real medical protocol critique or reenactment.

Do not copy real disaster triage cases / victims.

### Ordinary habit

conversationでも優先順位をつけて聞く癖があるが、最後に必ず:

> 「他に言えてないことは？」

と一度だけ聞く。

That question can later become an emotional fault line.

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Asa care / rights / resource side
- Yui post-emergency care consequence

Secondary:

- Tomori distribution Third Option

### Avoid

- doctor villain cliché
- “math is evil”
- actual medical misinformation

## 4.6 サエ — Care network organizer

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **世話焼きで頼れる人 / care burdenを分散するため他人の役割まで決める優しい圧力**

### Core belief

> 「一人に全部背負わせたら、いつかその人も倒れる。」

### Fear

> unpaid / family / community care being invisible until caregiver collapse.

### Violation

> household members / friends / neighborsへ、本人の意思より先に`あなたはこれを担当`とcare roleを配る。

### Ordinary habit

Party後に誰にも言わず片付け始めるが、途中から:

> 「あなた皿、あなた布巾、あなた残りしまって」

と自然に人を動かす。

This can be charming before it becomes coercive.

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Asa care / family-rights side
- Tomori household / reconstruction

Secondary:

- Ritsu / Kaname / Nemu counterpoints

### Avoid

- motherly woman stereotype only
- care = female duty
- abuse backstory auto-add

## 4.7 ハルマ — Labor / maintenance scheduler

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **現場を壊さないムードメーカー / 人の時間を最適化しすぎる怖さ**

### Core belief

> 「休ませるなら、止まらない順番まで組んどかないとな。」

### Fear

> one person's overload triggers a chain collapse.

### Violation

> rest / shift / task assignmentを本人のchoiceよりsystem continuity優先で固定する。

### Ordinary habit

誰かが欠伸すると冗談を言いながら椅子を押し出す。

> 「はい、十五分。戻ってこなかったら三十分。」

Initially lovable.

Later danger:

> “休ませている”のに本人が自分の時間を選べない。

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Michiru industrial / labor side
- Asa Android / Robot labor side

Secondary:

- Nagi unstable-employment carryover

### Avoid

- boss/foreman caricature
- hard work = virtue only
- alcohol/tobacco assignment before age review

## 4.8 ミノリ — Food / material logistics optimist

Status: `S2 ENEMY CANDIDATE`

### Attachment lane

> **何でも届かせる明るい物流屋 / yieldの低い場所を後回しにする笑顔の怖さ**

### Core belief

> 「余らせるくらいなら、必要なところへ回そう。」

### Fear

> food / fuel / material wasted while another place lacks it.

### Violation

> delivery efficiency / loss rate / number servedを優先し、small / remote / difficult householdを“次便”へ送り続ける。

### Ordinary habit

食事の残量と人数を一瞬で見て、本人も気づかない速さで取り分ける。

### S2 incident fit

Primary:

- Tomori `空欄世帯事件` / material distribution

Secondary:

- Yui delivery / public-service allocation consequence

### Fan appeal

- cheerful
- excellent at finding substitutes
- makes genuinely good group meals
- hates seeing food wasted

This makes conflict at Dream Party especially strong:

> Dream food is abundant, but Minori still automatically portions it.

### Avoid

- scarcity trauma auto-add
- rural/region stereotype
- “cheerful = shallow”

- **ナナセ** — access route
- **ヨシノ** — environmental / future reserve

Internal conflict:

> Isana wants one coherent plan.
> Nanase wants routes that remain usable.
> Yoshino wants consumption / growth limits.

They can oppose each other while all still create sacrifice.

## Asa — rights / care / body / resource

Strong:

- **ケイ** — care allocation

Important:

Question shifts from:

> who counts as a person?

to:

> **when personhood is recognized, who receives care / maintenance / time / work protection?**
