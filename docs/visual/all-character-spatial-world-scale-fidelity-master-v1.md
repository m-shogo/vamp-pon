# All Character Spatial / World-Scale Fidelity Master v1

Status: CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY
Scope: all 36 character designs and all production character asset kinds where a built or natural environment establishes scale.

## Purpose

Keep characters physically believable inside Yoru-no-Shirube's spaces. A character's body, species, age, mobility equipment, props and clothing must agree with doors, stairs, chairs, tables, beds, shelves, paths, vehicles, terrain and other spatial reference objects.

The world may frame a character differently; it may not resize, normalize or redesign them to fit a convenient composition.

## Authority boundary

This Master is subordinate to source-backed identity, body, species, mobility, appearance and world-place authorities. It translates those authorities into spatial relationships. It does not invent exact height, wheelchair dimensions, furniture standards, room plans or accessibility features when the project has not established them.

Unknown dimensions use conservative relative scale and composition. `OPEN` is not permission for the image model to invent a precise measurement or a new body size.

## Spatial invariants

1. **body-to-world scale family** — adult, child, older adult, plus-size/broad body, dog, cat, small robot and other body categories retain distinct relative scale cues.
2. **head-to-environment relation** — door handles, counters, windows and furniture may not imply a contradictory age/height category.
3. **shoulder / torso clearance** — passages, seats and workspaces must visually accommodate the established body without slimming or clipping it.
4. **mobility equipment clearance** — wheelchair or other authorized mobility equipment remains spatially plausible around doors, paths, tables and turning areas; do not erase it to make the composition fit.
5. **seat interaction** — chair/bench/bed scale must support the actual body category and posture; seating cannot shrink or enlarge the character.
6. **work-surface relation** — tables, counters, benches, shelves and tools should meet hands/body at plausible heights for the depicted pose.
7. **foot / paw / wheel contact** — ground plane, step heights, curbs, thresholds and slopes must match the body's locomotion/contact system.
8. **prop relative scale** — named props keep their source-backed scale relationship to the body even when the camera or room changes.
9. **storage / reach envelope** — pockets, shelves, hooks, bags and containers should lie within a believable reach/access route.
10. **architecture continuity** — repeated spatial anchors should not jump scale from asset to asset merely to beautify the character.
11. **perspective consistency** — vanishing point and lens effects may not make foreground architecture and character use incompatible scale systems.
12. **species continuity** — dogs/cats/robots remain their actual species/construct scale category relative to furniture and people; do not humanize them through room scale.
13. **age coding continuity** — children are not lengthened to fit adult architecture and adults are not child-coded by oversized furniture for cuteness.
14. **body-mass continuity** — broad/soft/plus-size bodies cannot be spatially thinned to fit narrow seats or passages.
15. **environmental wear/contact** — scuffs, tracks, floor wear and touched surfaces should occur at plausible contact heights/routes when visible.
16. **camera crop honesty** — a crop may omit spatial evidence, but it may not imply a contradictory unseen body/world scale.

## Built-world interaction rules

- Door, handle, rail, stair, shelf and table heights are scale evidence, not decorative lines.
- A seated pose must explain where body mass, feet/paws/wheels, back support and props go.
- A work pose must preserve usable reach, tool clearance and a believable work surface.
- If a mobility device is present, circulation and contact should be plausible enough that the image does not imply impossible placement.
- If exact architecture is unresolved, choose a neutral permissive arrangement rather than inventing a canonical accessibility solution or obstacle.
- Spatial adaptation should happen in composition and world layout before changing the character body.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- shrinking or slimming a character so they fit a chair, doorway or frame
- widening shoulders/lengthening legs to make a heroic doorway composition
- adult-height counters used for a child with no believable reach solution
- oversized furniture used to make an adult look cute/young
- tiny furniture used to make a character look powerful
- wheelchair disappearing because a doorway or crop is inconvenient
- wheelchair clipping through tables, stairs, curbs or walls
- dog/cat scaled like a mascot toy when canon says actual animal
- dog/cat scaled like a human companion silhouette
- small robot enlarged to human body scale for composition
- inconsistent prop-to-body size between reference and cut-in
- impossible stair rise/step relation used only for dramatic posing
- feet/paws/wheels floating above or sinking through the ground plane
- hands reaching shelves/tools beyond plausible arm/body relation
- furniture clipping through garments, body or mobility equipment
- architecture perspective using a different vanishing logic from the character
- background doors/windows changing scale to make the character taller or smaller
- invented platform/box/stool solely to solve an unresolved reach problem
- invented ramp/elevator/accessibility device presented as canon without authority
- invented vehicle, saddle or carrier to reposition a non-human character
- crop hiding a known mobility/body feature and then implying a contradictory body
- same generic room scale used for every era/place despite world-place authority
- decorative giant books, lamps, paper or keys used as world motif without spatial function
- fantasy-size props used to inject Yoru-no-Shirube atmosphere

## Yoru-no-Shirube world rule

World identity should come from believable use of space: where people wait, store things, reach light sources, protect paper, repair objects, move through thresholds and leave contact traces. Project motifs should have real scale and function.

A lamp is not automatically giant because light is important. Paper is not automatically oversized because records matter. Keys are not automatically monumental because return/closure matters. Symbolism may influence composition only after physical scale remains coherent.

## Unknown dimension rule

When exact body height, architectural dimension, furniture standard, equipment clearance or reach is not established, do not invent a number. Preserve relative category and use composition that does not require a precise unsupported measurement.

## Production rule

Production candidate generation must load this Master. Generated spatial relationships are candidate composition evidence only. A generated chair height, room width, doorway, ramp, vehicle, body measurement or prop scale accident does not create canon.
