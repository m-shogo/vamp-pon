# All Character Environment / Weather Fidelity Master v1

Status: CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY
Scope: all 36 character designs, all production character asset kinds where the character is visually represented.

## Purpose

Keep every character physically embedded in Yoru-no-Shirube's world without allowing rain, wind, dirt, cold, humidity, night atmosphere or cinematic weather to redesign the person.

Environment is not a decorative filter. It is a causal system acting on already-authorized body, hair/fur/shell, clothing construction, materials, storage, props, footwear and mobility equipment.

## Authority order

This Master is subordinate to identity, Living Visual, garment, color, world-material and source-backed appearance authorities. It may describe how an authorized thing reacts to an environment; it may not invent a new wardrobe item, body modification, exposure state, hairstyle, prop, mobility solution or signature weather behavior.

`OPEN` means omit or stay conservative. It does not mean the image model may decide.

## Environmental response invariants

1. **material response** — wetness, dust, mud, frost, heat and humidity must follow the actual material rather than a universal glossy shader.
2. **garment construction** — seams, hems, closures, pockets and layered overlaps determine where water/dirt catches and how cloth deforms.
3. **hair / fur / shell identity** — weather may alter clumping, lift, surface sheen or local displacement but may not create a new haircut, fur volume, species coding or shell construction.
4. **skin / body identity** — lighting, cold, heat or wetness may not whiten, tan, slim, sexualize, de-age or erase source-backed body traits.
5. **footwear / ground contact** — mud, water, dust, snow or wet flooring must agree with sole contact, tread/shape and movement.
6. **mobility equipment** — wheels, supports, canes or other authorized equipment remain present and respond physically to terrain/weather; environment cannot erase them for composition.
7. **prop protection** — paper, lamps, keys, tools, maps and storage respond according to known construction; protection may only appear when already authorized or structurally implied by an existing container.
8. **storage continuity** — weather does not teleport objects out of pockets or add new waterproof pouches/straps.
9. **color identity** — wet/dim/cold conditions may shift perceived value/chroma locally but cannot replace source-backed palette identity.
10. **light-source causality** — wet reflections, rim light, mist glow and highlights require an actual light/environment source.
11. **wear history continuity** — rain/mud/dust interacts with existing repair, wear and maintenance logic rather than painting generic grime everywhere.
12. **silhouette continuity** — wind and wetness may deform secondary cloth/hair within material limits but may not create giant capes, heroic wind shapes or a new silhouette identity.
13. **exposure boundary** — wet cloth, heat, wind or damage may not increase skin exposure beyond the Living Visual profile.
14. **age / species / body-state continuity** — weather never changes the character category for drama or beauty.

## Weather and environment channels allowed

Only when supported by the depicted environment:
- cloth darkening from absorbed moisture
- localized wet sheen on suitable surfaces
- heavier drape from saturation
- wind displacement within real cloth/hair mass
- dust accumulation on exposed lower/contact surfaces
- mud splash following direction and height of contact
- condensation/fogging on plausible glass/metal surfaces
- breath condensation in sufficiently cold air
- small posture adjustment to footing, cold, wind or rain when it does not invent a signature habit
- water droplets following gravity and surface geometry
- fur clumping or flattening appropriate to wetness
- wheel/sole track evidence when terrain supports it
- metal temperature/condensation response where visible
- paper curling/softening only when actually exposed

## Forbidden environment shortcuts

- universal cinematic wet-look skin
- transparent wet clothing used to create new exposure
- rain used as an excuse for sexualized clinging cloth
- automatic wind-blown hero hair
- automatic giant fluttering coat/cape
- invented scarf, hood, poncho, umbrella, gloves or boots because weather is present
- invented waterproof harnesses, thigh pouches or straps
- generic mud/grime painted uniformly over the entire character
- clean footwear in deep mud without a source-backed reason
- dry paper/map while visibly exposed to heavy rain without protection
- floating raindrops/snow used as the only evidence of weather while materials remain unaffected
- neon wet reflections with no light source
- blue night filter that replaces skin or costume palette
- cold weather whitening skin
- heat used to authorize opening closures or removing layers
- wind opening clothing beyond authorized exposure
- weather removing wheelchair/mobility/contact equipment
- weather turning dogs/cats/robots into human-like fashion poses
- soot/fog/rain hiding face/body mismatches
- adding tears/sweat/blush merely because the atmosphere is emotional
- enlarging eyes/highlights to compensate for dark environment
- adding fantasy particles/star motifs instead of material response

## World-fit rule

Yoru-no-Shirube world motifs should emerge through physical behavior: paper protects or degrades, ink bleeds only where it can, lamps illuminate nearby wet surfaces, metal dulls or reflects according to finish, repaired cloth reacts at seams and patches, storage choices determine what survives weather.

Do not paste stars, glowing paper, magical fog or gold highlights onto a character merely to make the scene feel like the project.

## Unknown environment rule

If exact weather, temperature, wind, surface condition or protective behavior is not established, default to a neutral physically plausible environment with minimal effect. Do not invent a dramatic storm, rain outfit, snow gear, sweat state or wind choreography.

## Production rule

Production generation must load this Master before candidate generation. Generated environmental reactions remain candidate rendering evidence only and never establish new wardrobe, material, weather habit, exposure, handedness, prop or canon.
