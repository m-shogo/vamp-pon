# All Character Footwear / Sole / Ground-Interface Fidelity Master v1

Status: `CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY`

Scope: all 36 characters, all 9 production asset kinds.

## Purpose

This Master prevents shoes, boots, sandals, socks/stockings interfaces and source-backed barefoot states from becoming generic leg-lengthening or fashion-beautification devices.

Footwear is a constructed object that must fit the authorized foot, carry load, flex at plausible locations, contact the ground and remain coherent with clothing, weather, mobility equipment and movement.

The governing chain is:

**Source-backed Footwear/Barefoot State → Authorized Foot Scale → Internal Volume / Last → Upper Construction → Closure → Sole / Heel → Ground Contact → Motion / Wear → Viewpoint / LOD Continuity → Candidate Review**

## Core rule

**Footwear follows the authorized foot and use context; it does not resize the body to fit a prettier shoe.**

Source-backed barefoot or no-footwear states are equally authoritative and may not be replaced with shoes for completeness, safety shorthand or premium styling.

## Unknown-footwear rule

Unknown footwear treatment defaults to:

`SOURCE_CONSTRAINED_MINIMUM_FUNCTIONAL_FOOTWEAR_COMPLETION`

This means:

1. preserve source-backed footwear existence, absence, class and major silhouette;
2. preserve authorized foot scale and lower-leg/ankle relationship;
3. complete only mechanically necessary hidden construction for an already-authorized footwear object;
4. do not infer heels, boots, sneakers, sandals, dress shoes, armor, socks, stockings, orthotics or decorative hardware from gender, role, rarity, era shorthand or personality;
5. generated hidden construction remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and never creates Canon.

## Foot-scale rule

Never change the authorized foot to fit footwear.

Do not:

- shrink feet for elegance;
- narrow the forefoot for pointed shoes;
- shorten toes to fit a compact toe box;
- lengthen legs by reducing shoe/foot volume;
- hide a foot-scale mismatch inside darkness or crop;
- change ankle thickness to make boots look sleek.

If a source-backed shoe and foot appear mechanically unresolved, preserve both and require human review rather than beautifying the anatomy.

## Toe-box / instep rule

The internal volume must plausibly contain the foot and any source-backed sock/stocking/orthotic layer.

A narrow visual silhouette does not authorize zero-volume toes. High boots do not erase calf/ankle volume. Tight shoes do not reshape the whole foot.

## Heel rule

Heel height and geometry are construction, not femininity or premium shorthand.

Do not add:

- high heels;
- wedges;
- raised platform soles;
- hidden heel lifts;
- elegant narrow heel shapes

without source authority.

Do not lower or broaden a source-backed heel merely because action art would be easier. Movement must adapt to the authored footwear.

## Sole / tread rule

A sole has finite thickness and a ground-contact surface.

Do not:

- erase soles for a delicate silhouette;
- add exaggerated tactical tread for action shorthand;
- add glowing sole edges for premium readability;
- change sole thickness by viewpoint;
- make the shoe hover above the ground;
- sink the sole through the floor.

Unknown tread remains simple and mechanically neutral rather than decorative.

## Closure rule

Laces, zippers, buckles, straps and other source-backed closures remain coherent with the Operational Access authority.

Do not add side zippers, elastic panels, hidden closures, extra straps or quick-release systems simply to make dressing easier.

## Barefoot rule

A source-backed barefoot state preserves:

- authorized foot scale;
- toe count/spacing family;
- arch/heel contact plausibility;
- ground contact;
- age/species/body-category consistency.

Bare feet may not be beautified by shrinking, lengthening toes, smoothing away structure or adding polish/ornament. Barefoot does not authorize anklets, toe rings or nail cosmetics.

## Movement rule

Footwear moves with the foot and ground.

- sole flex occurs at mechanically plausible regions;
- a rigid sole does not bend like cloth;
- boots do not collapse through the ankle unless material/construction allows it;
- running does not turn dress footwear into athletic footwear;
- crouching preserves heel/forefoot geometry;
- stairs/slopes preserve contact rather than floating.

## Seated / wheelchair rule

Seated feet retain footwear volume and relation to the body/equipment.

Where a wheelchair footplate is source-relevant:

- sole/footplate contact remains plausible;
- shoes do not clip through the plate/frame;
- feet/shoes are not resized to fit the composition;
- straps or footwear are not invented as disability accommodations.

## Weather / maintenance rule

Weather affects existing materials and ground contact.

Rain may wet a source-backed shoe according to material authority. Mud/snow may contact plausible exposed surfaces. Generated dirt, wear or scuffs do not become Canon and may not be used as personality shorthand.

Do not replace footwear by season unless an authorized variant delta does so.

## Premium rule

Premium rendering may improve material and construction clarity only.

Prohibited premium defaults include:

- higher heels;
- longer legs through reduced foot volume;
- pointed toe beautification;
- gold buckles;
- gemstones;
- extra straps;
- glowing sole/tread;
- polished patent-like gloss;
- thinner soles for elegance;
- oversized tactical soles for power;
- decorative ankle chains.

## LOD / chibi / sprite rule

Simplify surface texture before changing footwear identity.

Preserve:

1. footwear existence/absence;
2. class and major silhouette;
3. foot/shoe scale relation;
4. sole thickness and heel family;
5. major closure route;
6. shaft/ankle opening height;
7. ground-contact orientation;
8. canonical asymmetry.

Do not shrink feet/shoes or enlarge them into mascot boots merely for readability.

## Production review checklist

Before accepting a candidate, verify:

- footwear existence/absence and class are source-backed;
- authorized foot scale fits inside plausible internal volume without anatomical resizing;
- sole/heel/tread/closure geometry is coherent across views;
- ground contact is real rather than floating/clipping;
- action and seated poses preserve the same footwear class and scale;
- wheelchair/equipment interaction preserves shoe and foot volume;
- weather does not replace footwear or create Canon wear;
- premium does not add heels, gold, gems, straps, glow or foot beautification;
- barefoot states remain barefoot without ornament invention;
- LOD preserves class/scale/sole/heel/closure before micro-detail;
- unresolved hidden construction remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Canon boundary

Generated footwear class, heel, tread, closure, orthotic support, seasonal replacement, wear pattern, scuff history or barefoot ornament never creates Canon by plausibility or repetition.

Human-reviewed authority is required for promotion.
