# Yoru no Shirube — World Material Translation Master v1

Status: **CURRENT_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY_EXTENSION**

Scope: character design / clothing / prop / material / wear / repair / image-generation translation only.

This master does **not** add new story canon. It translates existing world authorities into visual construction rules so the image model cannot reduce Yoru no Shirube to generic fantasy/gacha decoration.

## Authority

Read with:

1. `docs/00-current-story-world-master.md`
2. `docs/visual/visual-design-production-master-v1.md`
3. `docs/visual/core5-era-life-design-master-v1.md` when Core5

If this master conflicts with current story/world canon, current canon wins.

## Core principle

**Worldbuilding must appear as consequence, not stickers.**

Do not communicate Yoru no Shirube by attaching stars, paper scraps, ink splashes, glowing gems, compass symbols or lantern ornaments to every character.

Instead translate the existing world grammar into:

- seam
- fold
- fastening
- panel division
- pocket/storage logic
- edge treatment
- repair history
- translucency where justified
- carried-object construction
- wear pattern
- reflected/emitted light behavior

## Existing world grammar → visual translation

### Night

Use through value hierarchy, practical visibility, local light response and material reflection.

Do not turn every garment black/navy or add glowing trim.

### Route / way home

Use through where tools are stored, how maps/keys/labels are accessed, how bags sit on the body, what is kept reachable, and how clothing supports walking/returning.

Do not add compass motifs to characters who do not own/use them.

### Paper / record / ledger / label

Use through folding, binding, stitched labels, replaceable tags, protected document pockets, worn edges, wrapping and archival handling.

Paper-like visual language must have a physical reason. Random floating paper is not character design.

### Black ink

Use through mark-making, written/recorded surfaces, stain history, edge contrast and printed/handwritten functional areas when source-backed.

Do not make generic black magical tattoos, ink veins or splatter costumes.

### Lantern / small practical light

Light belongs to actual lamps, tools, windows, indicators or source-backed phenomena. The object casting light must have a location, fuel/power implication or world function.

Do not convert accent colors, jewelry, eyes, seams and hems into constant glow merely to look premium.

### Old / changing star charts

Use through navigation/record interfaces, chart folds, measured lines, reference marks, viewing/reading behavior and source-backed objects.

Do not cover clothing with star prints or constellation jewelry by default.

### Memory / missing record

Use through absence, incomplete labels, erased/replaced traces, preserved old repairs, mismatched-but-explained replacement parts or protected empty spaces when source-backed.

Do not create random torn clothing, missing sleeves or asymmetry solely as metaphor.

### Repair / binding / folding

Use real repair logic: patch placement follows damage/use; reinforcement follows stress; binding joins actual layers; old hardware may be reused if character history supports it.

Repair is not a universal aesthetic. Characters who replace rather than repair should not inherit Tomori-like patch language.

## Material questions required before generation

For each major garment or prop, resolve:

- what is it made from?
- how heavy is it?
- how stiff is it?
- how does it fold?
- what reflects light and what stays matte?
- where does it wear first?
- can this person repair it?
- would this Era repair or replace it?
- what closure technology is plausible for the character/Era?
- where are frequently used objects stored?
- can the character sit, crouch, walk and use their signature prop in it?
- which world trace is present because of actual use rather than decoration?

## Character-specific rule

World grammar never overrides personal taste.

If the character dislikes jewelry, the world does not justify a star pendant.
If the character avoids exposed skin, Dream styling does not create cutouts.
If the character uses few items, the world does not justify belts and pouches everywhere.
If an Era has a different material/repair culture, preserve that difference.

## Core5 material translation

These are translations of existing Living Visual / Era / Appearance authorities, not new wardrobe canon.

### Yui
- soft, wearable, movement-friendly cloth remains primary
- lantern hardware is local and functional
- paper/record language appears in reachable protected small storage or subtle construction, not luxury print
- small soot/use traces may exist around actual lantern handling
- world styling may not turn Yui into a blue-and-gold protagonist costume

### Asa
- light, firm, fast-response construction
- labels/name-related language should be functional, replaceable, readable as handling systems
- asymmetry comes from access and construction, not extra fantasy flaps
- metal remains minimal and purposeful
- world styling may not turn sharpness into armor, harness density or aggressive piercings

### Nagi
- opaque, controlled, closed construction
- box/key logic appears through secured compartments, closure rhythm and narrow geometry
- silver/metal remains quiet and local
- world styling may not force glowing moon ornament, translucent cloth or exposed skin

### Michiru
- breathable travel cloth, movement-safe joints and practical map/compass access
- travel wear appears at believable friction/handling points
- route language appears through actual navigation tools and storage
- world styling may not become generic adventurer belts, explorer costume or star-map print overload

### Tomori
- repairable tough cloth, functional hardware and real access to tools
- repairs show cause, sequence and continued usability
- reused parts can differ slightly when the history explains them
- soot/heat/wear belong near work contact, not as decorative grunge everywhere
- world styling may not become steampunk cosplay, gear motifs or decorative tool-belt density

## Anti-generic world drift

Forbidden as automatic Yoru shorthand:

- universal star jewelry
- universal paper scraps
- universal glowing trim
- universal blue-black palette
- universal gold edge
- constellation embroidery on everyone
- decorative compasses
- decorative lantern pendants
- ink tattoos as mystery shorthand
- random torn hems
- unexplained patchwork
- floating cloth strips
- extra belts/pouches without stored objects
- premium metallic material replacing ordinary lived material

## Image-generation gate

Before a production character image is considered generation-ready:

1. world master has been read
2. character Living Visual Profile has been read
3. Era translation has been read when applicable
4. primary garment materials are resolved
5. closures and storage are resolved
6. wear/repair behavior is resolved
7. world motifs are translated into construction/use rather than pasted decoration
8. world grammar does not override personal exposure/body-modification preferences
9. generic fantasy/gacha filler is not filling unresolved design
10. emitted light has a source and does not replace color/material identity
11. props have physical body/storage relationships
12. unresolved world/material questions are not delegated to the image model

**OPEN means return to design, not let the model improvise.**
