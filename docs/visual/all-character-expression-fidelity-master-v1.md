# All Character Expression Fidelity Master v1

Status: CURRENT_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY_EXTENSION
Scope: all 36 characters.

## Purpose

Keep the same person recognizable through neutral, smile, laugh, concern, anger, pain, crying, surprise, Dawn/Kokuyou intensity and cut-in acting. Expression may deform soft tissue and posture; it may not silently redesign the base face.

This Master does not invent a character's preferred emotion, frequency of smiling, romance signal, trauma response or relationship behavior. Those remain governed by character/relationship/acting sources.

## Base-face invariant

Before expression, preserve the loaded identity authority for:
- skull/head width and length impression;
- forehead and hairline relationship;
- brow baseline and brow-eye spacing;
- eye socket placement and inter-eye spacing;
- eye construction and eyelid architecture;
- nose length, width and projection impression;
- cheek mass / malar fullness;
- mouth width and resting placement;
- philtrum/chin relationship;
- jaw width, taper and mandibular mass;
- age coding;
- asymmetry and source-backed permanent marks.

Expression acts on this base. It does not replace it.

## Allowed expression deformation

Expression may change:
- brow angle/height within the existing brow architecture;
- upper/lower eyelid openness and compression;
- cheek lift/compression;
- nasolabial and age-appropriate soft-tissue folds;
- mouth corner position;
- lip opening/compression;
- jaw opening;
- head tilt and neck engagement;
- gaze direction;
- tears, moisture and temporary flush when context-authorized;
- hair movement caused by body motion or environment.

Expression does not authorize:
- changing eye type or enlarging eyes into a common anime expression base;
- sharpening/rounding jaw to make anger/cuteness read;
- shrinking nose for beauty;
- changing face width to intensify drama;
- de-aging during smiles;
- masculinizing/feminizing anatomy by emotion;
- adding blush as automatic romance/femininity shorthand;
- adding freckles, beauty marks, scars, piercings or tattoos;
- removing wrinkles, softness, fullness or disability-related visible equipment;
- changing skin/fur/shell identity color;
- adding generic fang/cat-mouth/chibi symbols unless explicitly authored.

## Emotion without same-face acting

Do not solve every character with the same expression library. Acting should be constrained by existing character/embodied/relationship authority when available. If exact expression habit is unresolved, use a conservative low-intensity expression rather than inventing a signature tic.

A smile is not one universal curve. Preserve differences in mouth width, lip mass, cheek movement, eye compression, age folds, dimples if source-backed, and habitual restraint if source-backed.

Anger is not automatic sharp eyes + clenched teeth. Preserve eye architecture and jaw. Use brow/eyelid/mouth tension without changing identity geometry.

Sadness/crying is not automatic beauty lighting + enlarged wet eyes. Tears do not enlarge eyes or erase age/body identity.

Surprise is not an excuse for circular eyes and tiny mouth if those shapes destroy the base identity.

## Asset-kind rules

### character_reference
Default expression should be neutral or mild and identity-readable. A reference candidate should not depend on exaggerated emotion to look like the character.

### sprite_sheet_180
Expression pixels may simplify, but head/eye/mouth placement must remain anchored to the same base. Do not use one shared chibi-emotion face template across the roster.

### normal_cutin
Expression intensity may rise. Base face geometry remains locked.

### dawn_cutin / kokuyou_cutin
State intensity may alter lighting, tension and acting. It may not make Dawn younger/prettier/whiter or Kokuyou sharper/thinner/more monstrous by rebuilding the face.

### emblems
Expression fidelity is not applicable; emblem canon governs.

## Expression fidelity review

For any face-visible candidate compare, in order:
1. neutralized head silhouette;
2. eye socket/inter-eye placement;
3. brow-eye spacing;
4. nose placement/proportion;
5. mouth width/rest center;
6. cheek/jaw mass;
7. age coding;
8. source-backed asymmetry/marks;
9. then expression deformation.

If the candidate becomes identifiable only because of hair/color/prop after the expression is applied, expression fidelity failed.

## Hard blockers

- expression changes base eye architecture;
- expression changes jaw/face width enough to alter identity;
- smile/cry/anger de-ages or beautifies away source-backed age/body traits;
- emotional state resolves gender ambiguity or changes gender presentation anatomy;
- blush/tears/marks are used as personality or romance shorthand without authority;
- Dawn/Kokuyou state redesigns the face;
- repeated cut-ins converge toward one premium-gacha face;
- generated expression accident is promoted to canon without human review.

## Production rule

Expression fidelity is subordinate to Character Identity Production Master and Appearance Generation Contract, and must be loaded before rendering a face-visible production candidate. Unknown signature expression habits are not image-model freedom. Generated expressions remain candidate evidence only.
