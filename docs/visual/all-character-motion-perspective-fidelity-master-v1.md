# All Character Motion / Perspective Fidelity Master v1

Status: `CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_FIDELITY_AUTHORITY`
Scope: all 36 characters, all 9 character asset kinds.

## Purpose

Preserve the same person when motion, foreshortening, camera angle, cloth/hair dynamics, prop inertia, crop, and impact staging intensify.

Dynamic composition may change pose and apparent overlap. It may not redesign identity geometry, body mass, mobility equipment, clothing construction, or prop scale.

## Authority order

This Master is subordinate to Character Identity Production Master, Appearance Generation Contracts, Living Visual, Garment Production, Embodied Acting, Color Production, and Expression Fidelity. It constrains rendering and staging only.

## Identity invariants under motion

The following remain identity-locked even under extreme action:

1. species and age coding
2. head-to-body proportion identity
3. shoulder / torso / hip mass relationship
4. limb thickness family
5. hand and foot scale family
6. mobility equipment geometry and body relationship
7. head and hair base mass
8. garment anchor points and closures
9. exposure boundaries
10. named prop real-world scale relationship to the body
11. habitual posture bias when readable
12. silhouette identity outside temporary overlap

## Allowed motion deformation

- ordinary joint articulation
- soft-tissue compression/stretch consistent with anatomy
- hair lag, lift, compression and overlap within source-backed hair mass
- cloth folds, drag, lift and compression consistent with material weight/stiffness
- temporary silhouette overlap caused by action
- camera crop
- moderate foreshortening that preserves believable relative scale
- prop swing/lag around an existing grip, strap, pocket or mounting point
- motion blur that does not erase identity-critical features
- impact staging that changes pose, not body design

## Perspective rules

Perspective is a camera effect, not a redesign license.

- Wide angle may enlarge nearer forms optically, but may not convert the character into a new body-type impression.
- Low angle may increase dominance, but may not broaden shoulders, lengthen legs, enlarge chest, or shrink head beyond believable lens projection.
- High angle may not child-code adults, thin broad bodies, or miniaturize mobility equipment.
- Close-up may crop body evidence; it may not infer a slimmer or younger unseen body.
- Cut-ins may use dramatic crop, but the visible face/head/hand/prop scale must remain compatible with reference identity.

## Hair motion

Hair may move; base haircut does not change.

Do not:
- add locks, bangs, ponytails, braids or volume for action readability
- convert short hair into long flowing action hair
- increase hair mass to create a premium silhouette
- hide source-backed face geometry with convenient action hair
- turn every character into the same windblown diagonal-hair composition

## Garment motion

Material behavior must follow the World Material / Garment Production authorities.

Do not:
- create floating cape panels that do not exist
- make heavy material behave like chiffon
- make rigid workwear behave like magical ribbons
- split skirts/coats farther than construction permits to expose legs
- open closures for sexier movement
- lengthen hems, sleeves or scarves for spectacle
- add straps purely to create motion lines

## Prop motion

Named props remain tools/objects, not VFX icons.

- scale remains compatible with storage and handling authorities
- grip/contact point must make physical sense
- inertia follows material mass
- straps/chains/cords may only exist if source-backed or already authorized
- no floating duplicate prop, orbiting object, enlarged weapon, or detached symbolic version for readability

## Body exaggeration prohibitions

Never use motion/perspective to:
- enlarge hands/feet into a different stylization family
- shrink the waist
- lengthen legs
- inflate chest/shoulders
- erase softness/fullness
- turn older bodies athletic-young
- turn children into long-limbed teens/adults
- masculinize/feminize anatomy for action intensity
- remove wheelchair or mobility aids to simplify pose
- humanoidize dog/cat/robot characters

## Asset-kind behavior

### character_reference
Neutral-to-moderate lens. No spectacle-driven foreshortening.

### sprite_sheet_180
Prioritize readable pose arcs but preserve body family; do not compensate with giant hands, heads, props or common chibi proportions.

### normal_cutin
Dynamic crop and moderate foreshortening allowed. Identity geometry remains reference-compatible.

### dawn_cutin
Light/motion may intensify; no angelic floating-cloth redesign, long-leg beauty perspective, or whitening/de-aging lens treatment.

### kokuyou_cutin
Ink/soot/impact may intensify; no monster-limb distortion, black-neon body redesign, or predatory same-face perspective.

### emblems
Character-body perspective fidelity is not applicable; emblem canon controls symbolic geometry.

## Unknown policy

Unknown motion habits, dominant hand, preferred action stance, prop flourish, dramatic camera preference, and hair/cloth choreography are not image-model freedom.
Use conservative physically plausible staging that preserves all higher authorities.

## Generation boundary

A generated dynamic pose does not create canon for habitual pose, handedness, body proportion, garment construction, prop scale, or signature motion.
