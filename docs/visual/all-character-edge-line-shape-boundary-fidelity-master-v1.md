## Purpose
Preserve character identity when line weight, edge simplification, anti-aliasing, outline treatment, crop, resolution, state transform, cut-in emphasis, sprite reduction, or premium rendering changes.

## Core principle
Edges describe already-authorized shape. They do not redesign it.

If a face, body, garment, hand/contact relation, prop, mobility device, fur/shell silhouette, or other identity-bearing form becomes unreadable, repair composition, crop, value hierarchy, silhouette, or authorized simplification first. Do not invent a new contour language to rescue weak readability.

## Preservation order
1. face outer contour and jaw/chin geometry
2. eye opening/spacing/tilt and brow geometry
3. age-bearing facial structure
4. body category and shoulder/neck/torso mass
5. posture and weight distribution
6. garment construction boundaries and closure routes
7. hand/contact and prop relation
8. mobility equipment geometry and body relation
9. fur/shell/non-human silhouette logic
10. secondary folds, seams and non-identity micro-edges

## Unknown default
Unknown edge treatment defaults to `IDENTITY_PRESERVING_NEUTRAL_BOUNDARY`.

This means:
- moderate, non-beautifying contour weight;
- no invented eyeliner, lashes, jaw sharpening, nose refinement, cheek slimming, tattoo/scar/freckle marks, white rim, glow outline, decorative hatch, or accessory boundary;
- preserve authorized geometry over aesthetic uniformity.

## Asset-kind guidance
### Character reference
Use enough edge information to audit face/body/garment construction. Do not use rendering tricks to conceal ambiguity.

### Portrait / cut-in
Cropping may increase local line clarity, but may not sharpen facial geometry or beautify age/body cues.

### Full illustration
Interior edges can be richer, but the outer silhouette and identity geometry stay locked.

### Sprite / small-scale
Prefer silhouette, value grouping and a few high-value boundaries. Do not enlarge eyes, shrink nose/chin, narrow body, remove wheelchair/tool structure, or add a bright outline to force legibility.

### Chibi
Proportional abstraction must preserve relative identity differences across the cast. Chibi is not permission to converge on one cute-face base.
