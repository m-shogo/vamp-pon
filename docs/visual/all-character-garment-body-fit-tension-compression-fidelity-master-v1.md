# All Character Garment-to-Body Fit / Tension / Compression Fidelity Master v1

Status: `CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY`

Scope: all 36 characters, all 9 production asset kinds.

## Purpose

This Master prevents clothing from becoming a second, hidden body-redesign system.

A correct body construction can still be visually destroyed when the image model draws every garment as if it were tailored for the same slim fashion body. The result is especially damaging for broad, soft, plus-size, stocky, older, child, seated and mobility-equipment-using characters because cloth can silently erase authorized mass, invent unsupported anatomy, or force the body toward generic gacha proportions.

The governing chain is:

**Authorized Body Construction → Garment Construction → Material Behavior → Ease / Clearance → Pressure / Tension / Compression → Pose Response → Visible Fold Result**

Never reverse this chain. A pretty fold pattern is not evidence that the body or garment should be redesigned to justify it.

## Core fit rule

**Garment fit follows the authorized body rather than replacing it.**

The same authored garment must remain mechanically coherent over the same authored body across front, 3/4, profile, rear, standing, seated, crouched, reaching, moving, premium art, chibi and sprite outputs.

A garment is not a painted body contour. It is a constructed object with material thickness, seams, closures, grain, weight, stretch limits, ease, layering allowance, pressure zones and movement requirements.

## Unknown-fit rule

Unknown fit defaults to:

`SOURCE_CONSTRAINED_MECHANICALLY_PLAUSIBLE_FIT_COMPLETION`

This means:

1. preserve authorized body construction and garment construction first;
2. preserve known material, closure, layering, exposure and mobility constraints;
3. complete only the minimum mechanically required ease, clearance, fold and pressure behavior;
4. prefer ordinary functional fit over fashion-illustration exaggeration;
5. do not infer body cling, waist suppression, sexualized tailoring, shapewear, corsetry, elastic, cutouts, slits, rolled sleeves, open collars or raised hems from attractiveness, rarity, role, gender presentation or premium status;
6. generated fit remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and never creates canon.

## Body preservation rule

Clothing may obscure body edges. Clothing may not authorize a different body underneath.

Therefore:

- loose clothing does not imply a thinner hidden body;
- close clothing does not permit unsupported breast, hip, abdominal, gluteal or muscular exaggeration;
- broad shoulders are not narrowed to fit a jacket pattern;
- a plus-size or soft body is not compressed into a generic slim silhouette unless an explicit garment construction and source-backed compression authority requires it;
- a stocky body is not lengthened with vertical fold tricks;
- older bodies are not made younger through impossible tailoring or smoothing;
- child bodies never receive adult sexualized fit cues;
- seated bodies preserve plausible torso, pelvis and thigh compression rather than being stretched or slimmed for elegance.

## Material causality rule

Fold language comes from **material + construction + gravity + contact + motion**, not from anime shorthand.

Heavy cloth:
- larger, slower folds;
- stronger hanging mass;
- less arbitrary body tracing.

Light cloth:
- smaller and more numerous folds only where construction and airflow support them;
- it still does not cling to every anatomical contour by default.

Stretch material:
- may transmit body shape only within authorized stretch and garment construction;
- stretch is not exposure permission;
- recovery and seam load remain visible constraints.

Wetness:
- may change weight, surface and local cling according to material;
- may not become a mechanism for sexualized anatomical revelation.

## Tension rule

Every visible tension line requires a plausible load path.

Valid origins include:

- closure load;
- shoulder or arm movement;
- waistband or belt pressure;
- seated hip/thigh expansion;
- knee or elbow flexion;
- strap or harness pull;
- a fixed seam meeting a moving panel;
- gravity acting against a supported point.

Invalid shorthand includes universal pinched-waist lines, universal breast underfolds, crotch tension marks added for anatomy emphasis, hip-accent lines, thigh highlight bands, or decorative folds that imply unsupported body geometry.

## Compression rule

Compression is local and causal.

A waistband, strap, seat edge or garment binding can create localized soft-tissue displacement when source and construction allow it. It may not reshape the entire body, erase mass, manufacture a narrower waist or create a new body category.

Pressure behavior must remain coherent across viewpoints and poses.

## Seated / wheelchair rule

Seated construction is not standing art with bent legs.

For seated characters preserve:

- pelvis-seat contact;
- torso and abdominal compression;
- thigh spread and cloth displacement appropriate to the authorized body;
- back-rest interaction;
- hem and outerwear accumulation;
- pocket and bag accessibility;
- strap routing;
- wheelchair frame, wheel, cushion and control clearance where applicable.

Never float clothing above a seat to keep it pretty. Never let cloth intersect the seat or mobility frame. Never slim thighs, hips or torso simply to make clearance easier.

## Movement rule

Pose changes cloth distribution, not garment identity.

When reaching, running, crouching, kneeling or twisting:

- panels may shift within construction limits;
- hems may move because of motion and gravity, not because premium/action art wants more exposure;
- sleeves may ride only as construction permits;
- closures remain the same closures;
- pockets remain the same pockets;
- layers remain the same layers unless an authorized state delta changes them;
- body mass remains the same body mass.

## Layering rule

Layered clothing must account for thickness and clearance.

Do not collapse underlayers into the body to preserve a narrow silhouette. Outerwear needs plausible allowance at shoulder, armhole, chest/ribcage, waist, pelvis and movement zones.

Layering may not be used to hide a body mismatch.

## Footwear rule

Footwear must fit the authorized foot scale and use pattern.

Do not:

- shrink feet for prettiness;
- narrow a toe box without source authority;
- add heels to lengthen legs;
- change sole thickness merely to alter stature;
- ignore orthotic, brace or mobility needs.

## LOD / chibi / sprite rule

Simplification removes secondary fold information first.

Preserve, in order:

1. garment silhouette and construction;
2. body-to-garment fit family;
3. major pressure / clearance zones;
4. closure and layering topology;
5. mobility-equipment interaction;
6. only then secondary folds and micro-tension detail.

Chibi and sprite outputs may not converge all garments onto one slim, tight, cute costume template.

## Premium rule

Premium means better execution, not tighter tailoring.

Premium output may not automatically add:

- stronger body cling;
- narrower waist;
- sharper anatomy folds;
- open collar;
- rolled sleeve;
- lifted hem;
- extra slit or cutout;
- wet-cloth cling;
- corsetry or shapewear;
- decorative belts or straps used to justify a body contour.

## Anti-shortcut rule

Never solve fit difficulty by redesigning another governed layer.

Forbidden repairs include:

- changing body proportions to fit clothing;
- changing clothing inventory to fit a pose;
- adding exposure to avoid clipping;
- hiding body mismatch beneath coats, skirts, hair, bags, darkness, crop or effects;
- moving pockets or closures because folds are inconvenient;
- shrinking mobility equipment;
- deleting assistive-device contact;
- turning impossible cloth intersections into stylized folds.

Use camera, pose, physically plausible cloth displacement, omission of hidden micro-detail, or human review before altering authored construction.

## Relationship to world design

Yoru-no-Shirube world character is expressed through lived construction, not generic symbols.

Garment fit should reveal:

- how a person actually wears and maintains clothing;
- how often-used storage pulls on a garment;
- how repaired material behaves;
- whether layers were chosen for comfort, movement, work, weather or habit;
- how straps, bags, tools and mobility equipment sit on the body;
- how fabric responds to the world's humidity, rain, dust, night air and repeated use.

World flavor must emerge from use, material and construction. Do not replace this with decorative stars, glowing seams, floating cloth, gems or fantasy harnesses.

## Candidate boundary

Generated garment fit is evidence only.

`CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`

A generated fold, compression mark, fit solution, seam displacement, rolled edge, open collar, body contour or pressure behavior does not become canon because it appears repeatedly or looks attractive.

Human review must confirm that the result preserves:

- authorized body;
- garment construction;
- material behavior;
- exposure policy;
- body-modification policy;
- mobility equipment;
- storage and prop relations;
- world-use logic.

## Production review checklist

- Does the garment fit the authorized body rather than silently replacing it?
- Are shoulder/ribcage/waist/pelvis relationships still the same person under cloth?
- Is ease plausible for the garment construction and material?
- Do folds have gravity, contact or movement causes?
- Do tension lines have real load paths?
- Are compression zones local and mechanically justified?
- Do belts and straps avoid manufacturing a new silhouette?
- Does seated clothing preserve body mass and seat interaction?
- Does wheelchair or assistive equipment remain fully functional and visible where required?
- Are underlayers given real thickness instead of being collapsed into the body?
- Does premium rendering avoid automatic cling, waist suppression and exposure?
- Do chibi/sprite outputs preserve the same fit family before micro-fold detail?
- Has any mismatch been hidden with crop, coat, hair, bag, shadow or effect?
- Is every generated fit decision still treated as `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`?
