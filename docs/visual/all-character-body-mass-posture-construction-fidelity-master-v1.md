# All Character Body / Mass Distribution / Posture Construction Fidelity Master v1

Status: `CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY`

Scope: all 36 characters, all 9 production asset kinds.

## Purpose

This Master prevents the image model from silently normalizing every character toward one attractive body template. Body identity is not an optional rendering preference. Shoulder width, ribcage and pelvis relationship, torso length, limb proportion, soft-tissue distribution, center of gravity, stance and habitual posture are identity-bearing construction decisions.

This authority does **not** invent unsupported character measurements. Existing canon, approved living-visual profiles, body-category authority, embodiment authority and source-backed references remain stronger. Unknown body geometry is not model freedom.

## Core construction rule

A character must remain one coherent embodied construction across front, 3/4, profile, rear, seated, crouched, moving, chibi, sprite, premium illustration and environmental scenes.

Preserve the same structural family for:

- head-to-body relationship
- neck length and thickness
- shoulder breadth and slope
- clavicle span
- ribcage width/depth
- torso length
- waist placement and compression behavior
- pelvis width/depth/tilt

- hand scale
- thigh / lower-leg relationship
- foot scale
- body-mass distribution
- soft-tissue distribution
- muscularity family
- visible age-bearing body cues
- center of gravity
- habitual weight distribution
- habitual stance width
- habitual shoulder carriage
- habitual spinal carriage
- habitual head/neck carriage
- ordinary seated posture
- ordinary crouch/squat behavior
- mobility-equipment relationship when applicable

## Unknown-body rule

Unknown geometry defaults to:

`SOURCE_CONSTRAINED_EMBODIED_NEUTRAL_COMPLETION`

This means:

1. preserve every already-authorized proportion and body-category cue;
2. complete only mechanically required geometry;
3. prefer ordinary neutral anatomy over fashion-illustration exaggeration;
4. do not infer thinness, muscularity, curves, height, long legs, narrow waist, broad shoulders or sexual dimorphism from role, rarity, gender, sexuality, age label, skin tone, costume, personality or market expectations;
5. generated completion remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and does not create canon.

## Embodied invariants

- Premium art does not make the body taller, slimmer or longer-legged.
- Importance does not increase heroic shoulder breadth.
- Feminine presentation does not require a narrower waist, larger chest or longer legs.
- Masculine presentation does not require broader shoulders, lower body fat or larger hands.
- Child characters do not receive adult proportions or sexualized mass distribution.
- Older characters do not lose age-bearing posture or mass cues for polish.
- Plus-size, broad, soft, stocky, small, tall, disabled or otherwise distinctive body categories must not be normalized toward a single median body.
- Perspective may foreshorten but may not change underlying ratios.
- Action pose may redistribute weight but may not change baseline body construction.
- Expression does not alter shoulder, torso or pelvis construction.
- Clothing may obscure body boundaries but may not authorize a new body underneath.
- Loose clothing must not be interpreted as proof of a thinner body.
- Tight clothing must not exaggerate anatomy beyond authorized construction.
- Wet clothing must not become a mechanism for sexualized body revelation.
- Damage does not expose more body than the exposure authority allows.
- Crop may not hide body-category mismatch as a solution.
- Lighting may not slim the body by removing authorized volume.
- Surface highlights may not manufacture musculature.
- Chibi and sprite conversion may simplify landmarks but must preserve body-category identity.
- LOD may remove micro-anatomy before it changes gross proportion.
- Seated poses must preserve torso/pelvis compression and body mass plausibly rather than lengthening the torso to stay elegant.
- Crouching must preserve thigh/calf/pelvis volume and joint clearance.
- Hands and feet must stay in the character's scale family rather than shrinking for prettiness.
- Wheelchairs and other mobility equipment are not optional composition objects; body geometry, seat relationship, reach and clearance remain coherent.
- Nonhuman characters must not be humanized through human body-template defaults.
- Generated body geometry never creates canon.

## Posture rule

Posture is the second face. A character's ordinary stance should remain recognizable even in neutral clothing and flat lighting.

Preserve source-backed differences in:

- forward vs rearward center of gravity
- open vs closed shoulder carriage
- upright vs relaxed spinal carriage
- head/chin carriage
- symmetrical vs asymmetrical weight bearing
- narrow vs broad stance
- hands held near body vs outward/action-ready
- seated compactness vs spread
- tool-oriented or object-oriented hand readiness

Do not convert every character into the same contrapposto, fashion-model S-curve, heroic chest-up stance, pigeon-toed cute stance or wide-legged action stance.

## Production review order

When body fidelity conflicts with presentation polish, preserve in this order:

1. existing canon body category and age
2. source-backed stature and head/body relationship
3. shoulder/ribcage/pelvis relationship
4. torso/limb proportion
5. body-mass and soft-tissue distribution
6. mobility-equipment relationship
7. habitual center of gravity and posture
8. hand/foot scale
9. clothing clearance and contact behavior
10. pose readability
11. lighting polish
12. cosmetic anatomy detail

## Generation boundary

All generated body, mass-distribution and posture treatments start as `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

Generated images do not establish:

- exact height
- exact measurements
- new body category
- new muscularity
- new disability state
- new posture habit
- new scars, tattoos, piercings or markings
- new exposure preference
- new gendered anatomy assumptions

Human review and stronger authority are required before any such change can be promoted.
