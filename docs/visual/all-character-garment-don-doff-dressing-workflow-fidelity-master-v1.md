# All Character Garment Don / Doff / Dressing Workflow Fidelity Master v1

Status: `CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY`

Scope: all 36 characters, all 9 production asset kinds.

## Purpose

This Master prevents clothing from being visually coherent yet physically impossible to put on, remove, adjust or use in ordinary life.

The governing chain is:

**Body / Mobility → Garment Construction → Openings / Closures → Donning Path → Layer Order → Reach / Grip → Adjustment → Wearing State → Doffing Path / Storage**

A garment is not complete merely because its silhouette, seams and material physics look plausible. It must also admit a believable human/nonhuman use sequence unless source authority explicitly establishes a nonordinary mechanism.

## Core rule

**Every garment must have a mechanically plausible dressing path consistent with the authorized body, mobility, construction and closure topology.**

Front, rear, seated, standing, premium, chibi and sprite assets all depict the same wearable object. Camera angle or style may not silently invent hidden zippers, elastic, stretch panels, magnetic closures, detachable sections or teleport-on logic.

## Unknown-workflow rule

Unknown dressing workflow defaults to:

`SOURCE_CONSTRAINED_MINIMUM_PLAUSIBLE_DRESSING_WORKFLOW_COMPLETION`

This means:

1. preserve existing garment construction, opening and closure authority;
2. infer only the smallest ordinary don/doff path mechanically required by the known garment;
3. do not invent hidden zippers, snaps, stretch, detachable panels, back closures, side openings, lacing, elastic, magic dressing or assistant dependency;
4. do not infer disability accommodations, caregiver assistance, sexuality, modesty preference or ritual meaning without source authority;
5. generated dressing workflow remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and never creates canon.

## Opening rule

If a garment cannot pass over the authorized body in its closed state, a real opening or authorized stretch mechanism must exist.

Do not:

- shrink the head, shoulders, hands, hips or feet to make a garment pass;
- enlarge a neckline in one view only;
- invent elastic or stretch after the fact;
- assume a hidden zipper merely because one would be convenient;
- treat decorative seams as openings;
- detach a garment panel without authority.

## Closure reach rule

A closure must be reachable in the wearing context.

- front closures are not automatically reachable if blocked by equipment or layers;
- rear closures may require flexibility or help, but helper dependency is not Canon unless source-backed;
- small closures require plausible hand/finger access;
- gloves, sleeves, mobility equipment and carried items affect reach;
- premium rendering may not relocate closures toward convenient or attractive positions.

## Layer-order rule

Layering has an order.

- an outer garment cannot be donned before a non-detachable underlayer if the opening geometry prevents it;
- belts, harnesses and bags must sit over/under layers according to their construction and use;
- collars, hoods, scarves and headwear need compatible sequencing;
- footwear and lower garments must have compatible passage clearances;
- a hidden layer may not be deleted merely because dressing order is difficult.

## Body / mobility rule

Dressing workflow must respect the authorized body and mobility state.

- broad, soft, plus-size, child, older or otherwise distinctive bodies are not resized to make clothing easier to don;
- seated dressing may change posture but not body proportions;
- wheelchair geometry may limit access and clearance;
- prosthetic, orthotic or assistive equipment may affect sequence, but image generation may not invent removal habits or accommodations;
- balance, reach and hand use remain source-constrained.

## Hair / head / accessory clearance

Head passage must account for authorized hair mass, ears, headwear and accessories where relevant.

Do not solve conflicts by:

- changing hairstyle;
- shrinking hair volume;
- deleting earrings/piercings/headwear;
- widening collars without authority;
- inventing detachable collars or hidden zippers;
- clipping hair through closures.

## Footwear rule

Footwear must admit the authorized foot.

- toe box, instep and heel passage must align with the construction;
- boots need a believable entry path, opening, stretch or closure;
- footwear may not shrink the foot for elegance;
- high heels, side zippers, elastic gore, pull tabs or lacing may not be invented to solve entry.

## Adjustment rule

After donning, garments may require ordinary settling or adjustment only where mechanically plausible:

- aligning collars;
- settling hems;
- distributing gathers;
- tensioning existing straps;
- fastening authorized closures;
- smoothing trapped layers.

Adjustment may not become a mechanism for redesigning fit, exposure or body shape.

## Doffing rule

Removal is the reverse mechanical problem, not a magical reset.

- wet, damaged or compressed garments may be harder to remove;
- closures must reopen through the same topology;
- stored items may need removal before doffing;
- bags, belts or harnesses may need to come off first;
- emergency removal cannot be assumed unless authorized.

## Storage / life rule

Dressing connects to ordinary life.

When source-backed, garment construction should remain compatible with its likely storage mode:

- hanging;
- folding;
- rolling;
- stacking;
- equipment-mounted storage.

Unknown storage remains neutral. Do not infer neatness, wealth, ritual, sentiment or domestic routine from a garment alone.

## Premium rule

Premium art may not make clothing easier to wear by silently adding:

- hidden zippers;
- magnetic closures;
- elastic panels;
- detachable sleeves;
- extra slits;
- wider necklines;
- open backs;
- rolled sleeves;
- shortened hems;
- decorative harness releases;
- magical fastening.

## LOD / chibi / sprite rule

Small-scale assets may omit the visual depiction of minor dressing mechanisms, but the hidden workflow must remain compatible with the full design.

Do not simplify every garment into an impossible pullover topology merely because closures are too small to draw.

Preserve:

- major openings;
- major closure location/type;
- layer order;
- footwear entry logic;
- major strap/harness release logic;
- mobility-equipment interaction.

## Production review checklist

Before accepting a candidate, verify:

- the authorized body can physically pass through the garment openings;
- closures exist where needed and remain reachable;
- no hidden fastener/stretch/detachable mechanism was invented;
- layer order is mechanically possible;
- hair/headwear/accessories do not require redesign to dress;
- footwear has a real entry path;
- wheelchair/prosthetic/orthotic/assistive-equipment interactions remain source-constrained;
- premium and small-scale variants preserve the same dressing logic;
- unsupported workflow remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Canon boundary

Generated donning, doffing, helper use, closure reach, layer sequence, storage habit, dressing accommodation or removal routine never becomes Canon by plausibility or repetition.

Human-reviewed authority is required for promotion.
