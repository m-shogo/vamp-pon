# All Character Contrast / Value Hierarchy Fidelity Master v1

Status: CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY
Scope: all 36 characters and all 9 production asset kinds.

## Purpose

Preserve character identity and world readability through luminance/value design. Night, premium rendering, state effects and small-scale assets may change atmosphere, but they may not solve weak design by crushing shadows, whitening faces, outlining everything or pushing every accent to maximum contrast.

## Authority boundary

This Master is subordinate to identity, Living Visual, exact color authority, night/light, surface/tone-mapping, crop/silhouette, focus/effects, material and environment authorities.

`OPEN` does not allow an image model to invent a value signature, glowing face, white rim, black costume mass or high-contrast focal accent. Unknown value design defaults to restrained identity-preserving separation.

Generated value/compression choices remain `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.

## Preservation order

When value compression is necessary, preserve:
1. species / age / body identity
2. face geometry
3. body mass and posture
4. garment silhouette and exposure boundary
5. hand/contact and mobility equipment
6. prop relation
7. dominant/support/accent hierarchy
8. material distinction
9. environmental depth
10. decorative contrast

## Night rules

Night is not black fill. Retain enough midtone separation to read body mass, major clothing construction, prop contact and mobility equipment. Do not lighten skin as compensation. Prefer controlled local separation, authorized practical light and background-value management.

## Premium/state rules

Premium, Dawn and Kokuyou assets may redistribute emphasis but may not:
- maximize global contrast automatically
- add universal white or neon rim light
- turn face skin into the brightest object by default
- crush costume/body into a single black silhouette
- create a new high-value halo or aura
- convert accent color into the brightest available value everywhere

## Forbidden shortcuts

- face whitening for focal hierarchy
- glowing face for readability
- universal white rim light
- universal neon edge light
- pure-black costume mass hides construction
- black-point crush hides body category
- black-point crush hides mobility equipment
- black-point crush hides hand contact
- clipped highlights erase pale facial planes
- clipped highlights erase paper/cloth difference
- dark skin lifted toward grey for readability
- dark skin crushed into background
- cool night turns skin blue-black
- warm light turns skin orange-gold
- age texture erased by bright key light
- body slimming through dark-side contour loss
- plus-size torso separated by false edge glow
- child face overlit into adult glamour portrait
- fur becomes one flat dark blob
- robot shell becomes one chrome highlight mass
- all metal receives brightest highlight
- all accents pushed to maximum luminance
- star/beast accent becomes automatic focal light
- prop made emissive only to separate it from hand
- wheelchair/mobility frame darkened into invisibility
- background crushed solely to isolate character
- foreground black mass hides unresolved anatomy
- vignette removes garment information
- cinematic chiaroscuro used regardless of character/material
- premium asset automatically increases contrast ratio
- Dawn defaults to white/gold value hierarchy
- Kokuyou defaults to black/neon value hierarchy
- sprite uses white outline because silhouette is weak
- small-scale asset uses glow instead of correct simplification
- grayscale readability fixed with invented accessory
- generated value accident repeated as canon

## Unknown-value default

Use `RESTRAINED_IDENTITY_PRESERVING_VALUE_HIERARCHY`:
- preserve readable midtones
- preserve established skin/fur/shell value relationships
- separate major garment masses without invented outlines
- keep highlights below clipping
- keep shadows above information loss where identity depends on them
- reduce decorative contrast before identity evidence

## Production rule

Production candidate generation must load this Master. Value hierarchy and contrast are downstream rendering decisions. Generated rim-light habits, black-point treatment, facial brightness, accent emphasis or contrast signatures remain `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and never create canon by themselves.
