# Core5 Garment Construction Master v1

Status: `CURRENT_VISUAL_PRODUCTION_AUTHORITY_EXTENSION`

This Master turns the Core5 Living Visual, Era Life, Identity Geometry, and World Material rules into production-ready garment construction. It does **not** create new story canon and does not promote `AUTHOR_CANDIDATE` material to user-decided canon.

## Core rule

A Yoru no Shirube outfit is not a fantasy costume with project motifs pasted on top. It is clothing a specific person can put on, move in, maintain, repair, store objects in, and repeatedly choose.

Worldbuilding must appear through **construction consequence**:

- seams and panel divisions,
- fastening logic,
- pocket and compartment placement,
- material weight and fold behavior,
- wear at real contact points,
- repair at actual stress/damage points,
- object access and interference,
- local light response from a real source.

Stars, paper, ink, route, memory, lanterns, boxes, keys, maps, and repair language are never universal decoration licenses.

## Required operational tests

Every production design must remain believable when the character:

1. puts the garment on and removes it,
2. walks normally,
3. sits,
4. crouches,
5. raises both arms,
6. reaches the signature prop,
7. stores and retrieves ordinary objects,
8. responds to weather appropriate to the Era/Life profile.

If a belt, strap, buckle, pouch, patch, metal plate, cutout, chain, or flap has no use/history, remove it.

## Core5 production translations

### ユイ
Soft matte travel cloth, rounded folds, short hooded outer layer, crouch-safe hem, reachable small-object and protected-record storage. The lantern side must stay snag-safe and soot/wear remains local to actual contact. Flat soft walking boots. No corset, gold trim, gem clasp, floating paper, star jewelry, cape, or random harness.

### アサ
Light firm cloth with clean angular breaks; compact asymmetric jacket and diagonal construction driven by access and movement. One fast repeatable closure path and fixed tool/label locations. Flat compact footwear. Sharpness must never become armor, harness density, decorative flaps, heels, or premium metal edging.

### ナギ
Matte dense opaque cloth, closed collar, long narrow controlled vertical shape, secure compartment logic and quiet small silver hardware. Repairs are neat and often preventive rather than theatrical. Key/box grammar is structural, not glowing moon ornament. No sheer fabric, chest opening, jangling chain, torn hem, or luxury brocade.

### ミチル
Breathable travel cloth and abrasion-tolerant materials, movement-open joints, walking-safe hem, adjustable sleeves, and direct map/compass access. Wear follows knee, hem, bag contact, pocket, toe, and heel friction. Route and chart grammar appear through navigation handling—not adventurer belt stacks, printed star maps, floating scarves, or decorative compasses.

### トモリ
Repairable work cloth, localized worn leather only at load points, functional/reused hardware, secure sleeves, and real tool-access geometry. Every pouch must correspond to an actual tool class. Repair placement follows stress, abrasion, burn, or old fastener failure. Repair language does not license steampunk gears, empty tool belts, universal grime, gemstone workwear, or random tattoos.

## Image-generation policy

Production image generation must load the machine-readable Master and the target character profile. `OPEN` or unspecified garment detail is **not** image-model freedom. A missing required construction decision blocks production generation or must be explicitly exploratory-only.

High-resolution rendering may reveal stitching, weave, wear, and repair detail that is already justified. It may not invent extra fasteners, straps, jewelry, metal trim, skin exposure, patches, pouches, or ornament to make the design feel more premium.

Generated images remain `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and do not create canon.
