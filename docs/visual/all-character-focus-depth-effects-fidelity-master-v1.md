# All Character Focus / Depth / Effects Fidelity Master v1

Status: CURRENT_PRODUCTION_VISUAL_AUTHORITY
Scope: all 36 characters and all 9 production asset kinds.

## Purpose

Preserve identity, garment construction, contact physics, props, mobility equipment and world-grounding when depth of field, foreground particles, glow, fog, ink, bloom, rim light, motion streaks or transformation effects are used.

Effects support the design. They do not replace readable design.

## Authority boundary

This Master is subordinate to identity, Living Visual, garment, color, night/light, hand/contact, motion/perspective, environment, spatial, occlusion and crop/silhouette authorities.

`OPEN` does not allow the image model to invent a focus language, glow source, aura, rim-light signature or effect motif. Unknown focus/effect choices default to restrained identity-preserving rendering.

All generated focus/effect solutions remain `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and do not create canon.

## Focus and depth invariants

1. **face-plane readability** — identity-bearing face planes remain readable when the face is visible.
2. **eye-shape continuity** — blur/bloom may not turn distinct eye geometry into a generic bright-eye shape.
3. **body-mass readability** — depth effects may not erase body category or shoulder/torso mass.
4. **garment-construction readability** — major closures, layer boundaries and silhouette-bearing panels remain understandable.
5. **hand-contact readability** — required grip/contact may not be hidden by blur, glow or foreground particles.
6. **prop-relation readability** — a signature or task-critical prop remains physically related to its hand/storage anchor.
7. **mobility-equipment readability** — required mobility equipment and controls may not be blurred/cropped/effected away.
8. **species readability** — non-human body geometry remains readable; effects may not humanize or mascot-simplify it.
9. **ground-contact readability** — required foot/paw/wheel/contact evidence may not disappear into fog or bloom.
10. **hair-volume readability** — depth blur may simplify strands but not rewrite hair mass.
11. **outerwear-volume readability** — coat/hood/skirt volume remains distinct from background/effects.
12. **color-authority continuity** — glow and grading may not replace the identity palette.
13. **light-source causality** — bloom/emission requires an authorized source or is omitted.
14. **foreground-effect transparency** — foreground particles/effects may not permanently cover identity-critical anchors.
15. **background-separation restraint** — subject separation uses value/depth/composition before invented outline glow.
16. **state-transform continuity** — Dawn/Kokuyou effects may intensify atmosphere but may not redraw anatomy or costume.
17. **small-scale readability** — at sprite scale, simplify effects before simplifying identity.
18. **premium-asset restraint** — premium cut-ins may increase polish, not effect density at the cost of design evidence.
19. **focus-plane honesty** — an unresolved hand, garment or mobility error cannot be placed out of focus and called solved.
20. **cross-asset effect continuity** — generated effect accidents do not become recurring character signatures.

## Preservation priority

When readability conflicts with effects, preserve in this order:
1. species / age / body identity
2. face geometry
3. body mass and posture
4. garment construction and exposure boundary
5. hand/contact and mobility equipment
6. identity-bearing prop relation
7. silhouette and color hierarchy
8. material response
9. background atmosphere
10. decorative effects

## Forbidden shortcuts

- universal ultra-shallow depth of field
- face-only sharpness while body identity is blurred away
- blur hides wrong body proportions
- blur hides wheelchair or mobility controls
- blur hides broken hand grip
- bloom hides clipping
- bloom merges eye shape into generic glowing eyes
- rim light redraws jaw or body outline
- neon outline invented for readability
- halo invented because character is important
- backlight makes skin uniformly pale
- glow replaces exact palette
- glow added without source
- all metal becomes emissive
- all paper becomes glowing magic paper
- star motifs become automatic floating particles
- fog hides ground-contact errors
- smoke hides missing feet/paws/wheels
- ink hides garment construction
- foreground petals/paper cover face anchors
- foreground particles cover hands or controls
- lens flare crosses identity-critical face region
- bokeh circles become character motif without authority
- motion streaks replace limb continuity
- excessive bloom erases material boundaries
- chromatic aberration fakes edge detail
- vignette hides cropped mobility equipment
- darkness hides occlusion errors
- premium cut-in adds aura to compensate for weak silhouette
- Dawn adds white/gold glow as default luxury language
- Kokuyou adds black-neon aura as default identity
- effect color becomes third costume color
- effect opacity removes prop relation
- effect density increases with rarity/importance automatically
- small asset receives giant glow for readability
- background defocus erases world-use evidence required by the scene
- generated effect accident repeats and becomes canon

## Unknown-focus default

When exact focus/effect direction is unresolved, use `RESTRAINED_IDENTITY_PRESERVING_FOCUS`:
- moderate depth separation
- readable face/body/major garment structure
- no invented emission or aura
- reduce effect opacity before hiding identity anchors
- keep critical hand/prop/mobility relations readable
- send stronger cinematic alternatives to human review as candidates only

## Production rule

Production candidate generation must load this Master. Effects are rendering decisions beneath character and world authorities. A generated aura, bokeh language, glow treatment, flare pattern or focus habit remains `CANDIDATE_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and never creates canon by itself.
