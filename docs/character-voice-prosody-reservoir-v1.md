# Character Voice / Prosody Reservoir v1

Status: **AUTHOR RESERVOIR / NON-CANON / NO CASTING FREEZE / NO BODY-GENDER-VOICE STEREOTYPE**

全36人について、具体的な声優・声の高さ・方言・声質をCanon化せず、**台詞の速度 / 声量レンジ / 間 / 感情時のリズム変化 / 沈黙 / 非言語音**を作者・脚本・演出用の候補として持つ。

## Coverage

36 characters × 6 axes = **216 voice/prosody anchors**

Axes:

- `BASE_PACE`
- `VOLUME_RANGE`
- `PAUSE_PATTERN`
- `EMOTION_SHIFT`
- `SILENCE_USE`
- `NONVERBAL_SOUND`

## Separate from wording

This layer does not decide the exact words a character uses.

- Address / Naming / Register handles how names, distance, and formality may shift.
- Directed speech handles addressee-specific language/temperature.
- Voice / Prosody handles how a spoken line moves through time and intensity.

The same sentence should be playable differently for each character without requiring a stereotyped pitch, accent, or vocal gimmick.

## What remains Open

- exact vocal pitch / register
- exact timbre
- exact speaking voice age
- exact accent / dialect
- exact first-person pronoun or suffix
- voice actor / performer casting
- singing ability
- medical voice condition
- microphone/audio treatment
- recurring catchphrase sound
- runtime audio playback behavior

## Hard boundaries

This reservoir blocks:

- gender / sexuality / presentation = pitch or vocal style
- large body = deep/loud voice
- small body = high/quiet voice
- older age = frail/slow voice
- skin tone / origin = accent
- disability = childlike, weak, inspirational, or medically narrated voice
- Robot/AI = flat or emotionless voice
- artificial body = beep/interface voice
- animal = Human dialogue translated into mascot sounds
- twins = matched or opposite voice by default
- quiet = shy, weak, secretive, or untrustworthy
- loud = confident, strong, extroverted, or aggressive

## Representation guards

- Gen / Hana: full vocal range remains available; no old-person frailty or maternal speech sound by default.
- Kaname: body size does not prescribe low pitch, loudness, intimidation, or grunts.
- Hiyori / Touma: skin/style does not prescribe slang, accent, loudness, roughness, or performance voice.
- Suzu / Io: pitch, prosody, timbre, pause, and nonverbal sounds are not gender-diagnostic evidence.
- Amane: access effort or breath is not automatic medical narration or inspiration framing.
- Noa / Rum: personal rhythm is not CPU speed, latency, speaker size, fleet synchronization, or appliance beeping.
- Kuu / Yomo: vocalization stays species-appropriate rather than translated Human dialogue or mascot sound buttons.
- Kai / Nao: twins may independently converge or diverge; no mandatory matched/contrast casting.

## Era boundary

Era can shape conventions and available speech references, but this reservoir does not invent an accent or archaic vocal style from era alone. If exact historical speech rhythm becomes important, it should be researched and promoted separately.

## Animation / audio boundary

These anchors can inform future voice direction, lip timing, subtitle cadence, portrait animation, or audio design, but **runtime audio is not promoted automatically**. No exact milliseconds, decibels, pitch range, DSP preset, or animation timing is Canon here.

## Authoring principle

**声の個性は「高い・低い」より、どこで急ぎ、どこで止まり、何を言わずに残せるかで作る。**
