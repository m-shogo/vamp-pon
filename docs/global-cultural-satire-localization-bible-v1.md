# 1. Main Reality remains Japan

Current main seriesのRealityは現実の日本。

海外向けだからといって:

- Japanese schoolをUS schoolへ置換
- yen感覚をdollar文化へ自動変更
- family relationをmom/dadだけへ単純化
- honorific /呼び方の変化を削除
- foodをpizza / burgerへ置換
- shrine / temple / bath /商店街等をgeneric Asian fantasyへ変換

しない。

必要なのは**context**。

Playerが文化を知らなくてもsceneの感情は理解でき、日本文化を知るほど追加layerが読めるようにする。

---

# 2. Satire target hierarchy

風刺対象の優先:

1. system / rule
2. custom / expectation
3. generation contradiction
4. institution behavior
5. technology side effect
6. family role pressure

Avoid:

- ethnicity itself
- nationality itself
- region itself
- accent itself
- poverty itself
- disaster victimhood
- disability
- age itself

Laugh at:

> **what people have normalized, not the people who were harmed by it.**

---

# 3. Universal question + Japanese vessel

各sceneは二層にする。

## Universal emotional question

Examples:

- 誰が決めるのか
- 子どものためと言いながら本人へ聞いたか
- 家族だから我慢するべきか
- 便利になったのに何が失われたか
- 安全のためならprivacyをどこまで渡すか
- 成功のためなら誰を置いていくか

## Japanese cultural vessel

Examples:

- school roster
- PTA / neighborhood relation
- company hierarchy
- commuter train
- entrance exams
- local shopping street
- family altar where household evidence supports it
- hometown dialect switching
- New Year / Obon / seasonal gift
- company drinking party
- public bath / local bath culture where appropriate
- convenience store / vending / cashless coexistence

海外Playerは上の問いでsceneを理解できる。
日本の生活を知るPlayerは下のdetailまで楽しめる。

# 4. Family-role localization

Japanese terms may encode relation position.

Examples:

- 伯父 / 叔父
- 伯母 / 叔母
- 祖父母
- 兄 / 弟 / 姉 / 妹
- 義理 / 養親 / 親代わり

English等で単に`uncle`へまとめる場合でも、Story上age / side / hierarchyが必要ならtranslator noteを持つ。

Do not invent a family relation merely because target language requires pronoun clarity.

---

# 5. Dialect localization

Japanese方言の価値はphoneticsだけではない。

Encode:

- intimacy
- hometown return
- emotional loss of control
- family hierarchy
- professional mask
- old friend familiarity
- generation

Therefore English localization should not map every Japanese dialect to one stereotyped rural accent.

Possible strategies:

- standard English + selected local vocabulary
- sentence compression / rhythm change
- formality drop
- idiom change
- nickname / family address shift

Translator must know `D0-D3` dialect visibility level from Character Reality Origin Bible.

---

# 6. Food localization

Do not replace Japanese food just because target Player may not know it.

Keep:

- 焼きおにぎり
- おはぎ
- いなり寿司
- 肉まん
- 山菜そば
- local / Era food

when Character identity depends on it.

Use:

- short visual context
- item illustration
- glossary on optional Lorebook

instead of script exposition.

Commercial product names remain generic where licensing / trademark dependence is unnecessary.

---

# 7. Place-name localization

Real Japanese place names remain real.

Examples:

- 荒川区 → Arakawa City / Arakawa-ku treatment decided by localization style guide
- 新宿 → Shinjuku
- local city / prefecture names retained

Do not transform real Japanese locations into invented Western cities.

For海外 marketing:

- readable Romanization
- optional map
- cultural note

can support understanding.

---

# 8. Pilgrimage value without advertisement writing

Strong commercial outcome:

> Player likes Character → Character loves ordinary place → Player wants to see that place.

Weak outcome:

> Character lists tourist attractions because marketing wanted collaboration.

Use:

- station they always miss the same exit at
- ordinary bridge
- shopping street food
- local library
- riverbank
- old tram stop
- neighborhood park
- family shop category / fictional store

Real business tie-ins come **after** Story fit and require separate legal / permission review.

---

# 9. Overseas cast expansion compatibility

Main Reality is Japan, but future Seasons / spin-offs may include:

- people born abroad who live in Japan
- mixed-nationality family
- migrant worker / student
- returnee
- international marriage household
- foreign tourist only when tourism itself matters

Do not add one foreign Character as a culture encyclopedia.

Each person still needs:

- own household
- own generation
- own class / work / school position
- reason for being in incident location
- language comfort
- friendships beyond nationality

This keeps overseas expansion possible without converting the main story into a world-culture checklist.

---

# 10. Skin tone and origin localization

- Brown / dark skin does not automatically mean foreign-born.
- Japanese-born Brown-skin Character is valid.
- nationality must not be inferred from skin tone.
- localization art may not lighten / darken skin for market assumptions.
- Hiyori / Touma remain visually distinct and culturally distinct.

If Hiyori receives a strong regional culture root and Touma receives a metropolitan craft root, that difference must remain visible in translations without treating either as a skin-color explanation.

---

# 11. Era satire translation test

Every translated satire line should pass:

1. Who / what is being criticized?
2. Is the target a system/custom rather than vulnerable person?
3. Is the line still Character-specific?
4. Does Present remain criticizable?
5. Does Old Era remain capable rather than ignorant?
6. Does Future remain strange rather than superior?
7. Can the joke survive without a footnote?
8. If not, can the visual / prop carry half the context?

---

# 12. Spoiler / Lorebook split

Main route:

- enough context to emotionally understand
- do not lecture cultural history

Optional Lorebook:

- place history
- Era background
- food explanation
- dialect note
- family term note
- cultural source note

This is especially useful for overseas Players who want deeper Japanese context.

---

# 13. Global commercial guard

Do not alter Canon solely for:

- tourism campaign
- region sponsorship
- product placement
- overseas distributor preference
- temporary trend

Collaboration should select a Character / place already compatible with Story.

**Story creates commercial value; commercial value must not overwrite Story truth.**
