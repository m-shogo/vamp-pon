## Inner canon

- Inner role: the observer who can focus on what others miss.
- Misunderstanding: seeing clearly means they must say everything.
- Warm reversal: some things should be seen first, then gently shown.
- Visual hook: round glasses, one lens catching pale light, slim forward-leaning observer silhouette.
- Prop: lens cloth, focus memo, round glasses.
- Gameplay hook: weakpoint exposure, accuracy support, blur removal.
- Story use: finds the edge of a memory Yui overlooked.
- Cutin direction: a round lens catches dawn-before-dawn light and brings a blurred paper edge into focus.
