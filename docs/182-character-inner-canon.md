# 182. Character Inner Canon

Purpose: keep the 20-character roster usable without forcing implementation now.

This document defines each character's inner role, visual hook, story use, and gameplay direction.
It is safe planning canon. Do not treat this as a requirement to ship all 20 characters in the first playable build.

Canon sources:

- `src/game/data/characterCanon.ts`
- `src/game/data/worldTerms.ts`
- `docs/180-unified-character-canon.md`
- `docs/181-character-canon-integration-audit.md`

## Shared rule

Each character must have:

1. a vessel or readable prop
2. a different silhouette
3. a personal misunderstanding
4. a warm reversal
5. one tie to Yui
6. one tie to a non-Yui character
7. one gameplay hook
8. one cutin image direction

Cutin images stay textless. Names, ranks, and titles are UI text.

## 01. Yui

- Inner role: the collector who thinks forgetting is her fault.
- Misunderstanding: she thinks she lost or abandoned things.
- Warm reversal: she divided memories to protect them.
- Visual hook: blue hood, lantern in right hand, right-shoulder strap to left-waist bag.
- Prop: full lantern.
- Gameplay hook: stable pickup range, small enemy conversion, memory fragment recovery.
- Story use: player lens and emotional center.
- Cutin direction: warm horizontal lantern light, paper fragments, small but decisive face.
- Future caution: do not make every fire character a Yui clone.

## 02. Asa

- Inner role: the one who gives names back.
- Misunderstanding: being unnamed means being unwanted.
- Warm reversal: names were withheld because losing them was feared.
- Visual hook: name tag, paper slips, neat readable silhouette.
- Prop: small name tag with ink-light.
- Gameplay hook: mark enemies, name-seal targets, strengthen marked conversions.
- Story use: turns anonymous shadows into people again.
- Cutin direction: paper tags and written marks rushing across the screen.
- Future caution: keep her different from Yui by avoiding lantern-forward visuals.

## 03. Nagi

- Inner role: the keeper who closes things to protect them.
- Misunderstanding: putting something away is the same as trapping it.
- Warm reversal: some things survived because they were kept closed.
- Visual hook: moon box, key charm, guarded posture.
- Prop: moon box.
- Gameplay hook: defense, storing danger, slowing pressure, close-range safety.
- Story use: explains why some memories should not open immediately.
- Cutin direction: keyhole light, moon-box lid, dark edges kept outside.
- Future caution: do not make her passive; her defense should feel intentional.

## 04. Michiru

- Inner role: the guide who reads the way home.
- Misunderstanding: losing the route means losing the destination.
- Warm reversal: the route was never the point; the person waiting was.
- Visual hook: compass pin, map lines, wind-swept route-reader shape.
- Prop: compass light.
- Gameplay hook: movement, route flares, safe paths, positional control.
- Story use: keeps the return-home theme visible in stages.
- Cutin direction: compass needle, map thread, stars connecting into a road.
- Future caution: avoid making her only a speed stat character.

## 05. Tomori

- Inner role: the repairer who keeps old light alive.
- Misunderstanding: if nobody waits, the light has no job.
- Warm reversal: the light stayed on because someone kept repairing it.
- Visual hook: repair lamp part, tool bag, soot-warm palette.
- Prop: repaired lantern part / repair lamp.
- Gameplay hook: sustained area control, re-lighting, field repair, setup power.
- Story use: proves Yui's lantern has history and maintenance.
- Cutin direction: repair seams, sparks, warm light through cracks.
- Future caution: do not make Tomori another full-lantern protagonist.

## 06. Sen

- Inner role: the teacher whose words remained after they vanished.
- Misunderstanding: words not heard were useless.
- Warm reversal: the children used those words to begin playing and surviving.
- Visual hook: chalk dust, teacher coat, blackboard-line gesture.
- Prop: chalk lamp.
- Gameplay hook: line drawing, route guidance, enemy path manipulation.
- Story use: an older voice that left instructions without explaining everything.
- Cutin direction: chalk lines, dust sparkles, blackboard edge.
- Future caution: keep the adult scale readable but not realistic.

## 07. Ritsu

- Inner role: the older sibling who leaves half behind.
- Misunderstanding: failing to keep everything means failing to protect.
- Warm reversal: the half left behind became a return marker.
- Visual hook: half-wrapper motif, split-color jacket, protective grin.
- Prop: half-candy wrapper light.
- Gameplay hook: split projectiles, shared pickup bonus, divided attacks.
- Story use: connects sibling warmth with the idea of partial memory.
- Cutin direction: one light splitting into two, candy wrapper shine.
- Future caution: do not make sibling drama too direct too early.

## 08. Koyori

- Inner role: the small child who wants her name written.
- Misunderstanding: being named later means being less important.
- Warm reversal: her name was written in the hardest place to erase.
- Visual hook: oversized small name badge, tiny helper silhouette.
- Prop: small name badge.
- Gameplay hook: helper summon, small fragment pickup, support recovery.
- Story use: makes the name theme smaller, kinder, and sharper.
- Cutin direction: tiny name tags floating like fireflies.
- Future caution: avoid making her too mascot-only; keep emotional weight.

## 09. Gen

- Inner role: the old guide whose memory still works.
- Misunderstanding: old roads are useless now.
- Warm reversal: only the old road still reaches dawn.
- Visual hook: old cap, brass compass, sturdy slow silhouette.
- Prop: old compass glow.
- Gameplay hook: low speed, high durability, safe-zone generation.
- Story use: gives the world age and makes memory useful, not just sad.
- Cutin direction: station bench, old compass, dim road light.
- Future caution: avoid making him a joke old-man archetype.

## 10. Hana

- Inner role: the preserver who saves things by putting them away.
- Misunderstanding: storing things means forgetting them.
- Warm reversal: stored things did not disappear.
- Visual hook: pressed flower, shawl, gentle elder shape.
- Prop: pressed-flower bookmark.
- Gameplay hook: trap bloom, slow field, damage over time.
- Story use: mirrors Nagi through preservation rather than sealing.
- Cutin direction: pressed flowers opening across paper.
- Future caution: keep flower visuals restrained and not too cute.

## 11. Yubi

- Inner role: the carrier of a letter that may never arrive.
- Misunderstanding: an undelivered letter failed.
- Warm reversal: even undelivered words moved someone.
- Visual hook: mail bag, sealed envelope, delivery route stripe.
- Prop: sealed letter lamp.
- Gameplay hook: delayed homing shots, late arrival burst.
- Story use: connects distant characters without direct meetings.
- Cutin direction: stamps, envelopes, light traveling along a route.
- Future caution: keep delivery theme emotional, not postal gag.

## 12. Madoka

- Inner role: the witness who thought watching was nothing.
- Misunderstanding: seeing without acting has no value.
- Warm reversal: noticing was the first rescue.
- Visual hook: window-frame motif, paper plane, quiet observer shape.
- Prop: window-paper plane light.
- Gameplay hook: reveal cone, scouting, hidden pickup/enemy visibility.
- Story use: allows secrets to exist without exposition.
- Cutin direction: window light, paper plane crossing a dark room.
- Future caution: do not make her passive; seeing should become action.

## 13. Shiro

- Inner role: the archive child who protects unread pages.
- Misunderstanding: unreadable pages have no value.
- Warm reversal: the unreadable page was the most important one.
- Visual hook: round glasses, white bookmark, bookish silhouette.
- Prop: white bookmark.
- Gameplay hook: XP conversion, archive bonus, collection support.
- Story use: becomes the bridge to the collection / 灯録 system.
- Cutin direction: white bookmark, page light, glasses catching a small glow.
- Future caution: this is the glasses character; keep the glasses readable in small sprites.

## 14. Tobari

- Inner role: the gatekeeper who leaves a return sign.
- Misunderstanding: only watching someone leave is useless.
- Warm reversal: the one who watches can preserve the return path.
- Visual hook: ticket punch, gate-line posture, station uniform accent.
- Prop: ticket punch light.
- Gameplay hook: gate lines, flow cutting, enemy route separation.
- Story use: ties travel, stations, and return into one symbol.
- Cutin direction: punched tickets, gate light, line opening and closing.
- Future caution: avoid modern train UI; keep it storybook-night.

## 15. Nemu

- Inner role: the sleeper who saw the route in a dream.
- Misunderstanding: waking late means missing everything.
- Warm reversal: the dream became the map.
- Visual hook: sleepy eyes, dream diary, soft water-like silhouette.
- Prop: dream diary glow.
- Gameplay hook: random memory roll, high variance upgrades.
- Story use: lets the story bend reality without explaining too much.
- Cutin direction: diary page becoming water, stars under the surface.
- Future caution: keep random effects controllable enough for players.

## 16. Kuroori

- Inner role: the folder who hides a page to keep it intact.
- Misunderstanding: opening things destroys them.
- Warm reversal: folding made the page portable.
- Visual hook: black origami, folded cape shape, sharp paper silhouette.
- Prop: black origami.
- Gameplay hook: transform shape, mode-changing attack.
- Story use: first bridge from memory protection into rival logic.
- Cutin direction: black fold lines, paper opening only halfway.
- Future caution: should feel rival-like but not pure villain.

## 17. Kage1

- Inner role: the shadow that hides marks to protect them.
- Misunderstanding: light exposes and destroys everything.
- Warm reversal: shadow preserved what dawn would have burned.
- Visual hook: shadow crease, close-range stance, dark folded scarf.
- Prop: shadow crease.
- Gameplay hook: close-range high damage with risk.
- Story use: questions whether darkness can also protect.
- Cutin direction: shadow crease becoming a short black slash.
- Future caution: avoid generic dark assassin; keep the protection motive.

## 18. Kage2

- Inner role: the eraser who pretends to remove dangerous names.
- Misunderstanding: leaving a name will hurt someone.
- Warm reversal: pretending to erase became an escape route.
- Visual hook: eraser shard, smudged name mark, blurred outline.
- Prop: eraser shard.
- Gameplay hook: debuff, weaken, blur enemy attacks.
- Story use: antagonist mirror to Asa.
- Cutin direction: letters fading, erased space glowing faintly.
- Future caution: do not make deletion equal evil; it is fear-based.

## 19. Kage3

- Inner role: the measurer who reads the angle of night.
- Misunderstanding: dawn means shadows cease to matter.
- Warm reversal: shadows make shape visible even in morning.
- Visual hook: night ruler, angle lines, precise tilted posture.
- Prop: night ruler.
- Gameplay hook: angle crits, directional damage, technical positioning.
- Story use: turns geometry into memory logic.
- Cutin direction: ruler lines, angular dark light, measured dawn crack.
- Future caution: keep mechanics readable; angle systems can become opaque.

## 20. Kage4

- Inner role: the blank-card holder who protects unfinished space.
- Misunderstanding: writing everything ends the story.
- Warm reversal: blank space lets someone continue.
- Visual hook: blank card, quiet empty-frame silhouette, pale dark accent.
- Prop: blank card.
- Gameplay hook: late growth, stored choices, endgame burst.
- Story use: protects sequel/future-page possibility without forcing it.
- Cutin direction: blank card frame, light appearing only on the border.
- Future caution: do not explain the blank too early.
