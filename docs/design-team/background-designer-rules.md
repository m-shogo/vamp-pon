# Background Designer Rules

Lead: 灯見 つむぎ
Mission: 背景を美しくしつつ、ゲームプレイを絶対に邪魔しない。

## Core rule

Backgrounds are gameplay surfaces, not illustration-only images.

## Shared background language

- night-blue base
- paper scraps
- faint map lines
- worn stone or desk texture
- small lantern light
- forgotten-object motifs
- low contrast by default

## Stage motif list

| Stage | Main motif | Texture | Gameplay note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| forgotten street | lamps, signs, stone | dark stone + paper | player must stand out |
| memory desk | notes, ink, tools | worn paper + wood | item visibility priority |
| night library | shelves, windows | dark wood + purple light | avoid busy book lines |
| drifting paper sea | floating notes | soft paper + stars | low obstacle noise |
| dawn hill | sunrise, map end | warm horizon | final stage clarity |

## Density rules

- Main gameplay area must be quieter than screen edges.
- Bright points must not compete with pickups.
- Decorative paper must not look like collectable items.
- Background texture must be visible at 4x but calm at 1x.
- Tile seams must not form strong lines.

## 80-point target

- The stage has a clear location identity.
- The player, enemies, bullets, pickups are readable.
- The background still feels like Vamp Pon.
- Details support story without becoming noise.
- The stage can repeat or scroll without obvious seams.

## Do not

- Do not brighten the background to make it pretty.
- Do not put high-contrast detail under the player.
- Do not scatter star-like lights that look like pickups.
- Do not make every stage the same dark-blue floor.
