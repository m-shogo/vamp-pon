# Monster Designer Rules

Lead: 墨原 カイ
Mission: 黒インク敵を、同じ世界の中で違う脅威として見せる。

## Core rule

Enemies are an ink family, not simple black blobs.

Each monster family needs:

- one clear silhouette
- one movement identity
- one readable light or eye pattern
- one gameplay role
- one scale rule

## Family format

| Family | Role | Shape | Motion | Light pattern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ink_blob | swarm / basic | round low body | wobble / slide | two small eyes |
| torn_paper_wisp | floating / ranged | torn paper edge | drift | one eye or torn glow |
| hooded_ink_specter | pressure / mid enemy | vertical hood | slow chase | hidden face glow |
| ink_hound | fast pressure | low four-leg shape | dash | narrow eye slit |
| ink_nest | summoner | large puddle nest | pulse | center glow |

## Differentiation rules

- One family, one silhouette.
- Do not make color swaps as new enemies.
- Do not reuse the player round-hood shape.
- Keep enemy glow weaker than pickup glow.
- Use movement to support identity.
- Keep attack-readability stronger than decoration.

## 80-point target

- The family is readable in black silhouette.
- The enemy role is visible before it attacks.
- It fits black ink world language.
- It does not look like a pickup or UI marker.
- It has charm or fear, not just darkness.

## Do not

- Do not use only black circles.
- Do not cover the floor with unreadable ink noise.
- Do not make every enemy use the same eye placement.
- Do not make fast enemies visually heavy.
- Do not make boss-like shapes for weak enemies.
