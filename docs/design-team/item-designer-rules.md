# Item Designer Rules

Lead: Kamiya Kotoha
Mission: Make items feel like story props, not generic symbols.

## Core rule

Every item needs a function signal and a story signal.

## Item categories

- memory shard: star or crystal shape, warm light, collect role
- memory capsule: bottle shape, sealed light, stored memory role
- travel record: notebook or map, stamp motif, progress role
- unfinished memo: torn note, paper clip, hint role
- lantern charm: small lamp, warm light, aura role
- ticket: purple paper, moon mark, travel role

## Shape rules

- Pickup silhouettes must read at 10px to 16px.
- Cards and books need worn paper edges.
- Bottles need clear glass outline and inner light.
- Stars must not all share the same shape.
- Reward items can glow; background props should not glow the same way.

## 80-point target

- readable at 1x
- clear function symbol
- touchable collectible feeling
- belongs to night / paper / memory world
- does not look like a UI icon or enemy eye

## Do not

- Do not make items generic stars only.
- Do not make paper items all the same rectangle.
- Do not use glow to hide weak shape.
- Do not make decorative props look collectable.
