## Address / Naming / Register

Source: `src/game/data/characterAddressNamingRegisterReservoir.ts`

Indexed status:

`AUTHOR_RESERVOIR_NON_CANON_EXACT_WORDING_OPEN_NO_NAME_FORM_AFFECTION_SCORE`

The index does not freeze exact first-person pronouns, suffixes, nicknames, dialect, legal/private names, or relationship labels.

Hard boundaries:

- nickname/name form != affection or romance score
- age/status != submission/superiority
- gender/sexuality/presentation != register
- origin/skin tone != dialect
- stable profile alias != spoken name
- correcting address != obligation to disclose identity history

## Voice / Prosody

Source: `src/game/data/characterVoiceProsodyReservoir.ts`

Indexed status:

`AUTHOR_RESERVOIR_NON_CANON_NO_CASTING_FREEZE_NO_BODY_GENDER_VOICE_STEREOTYPE`

The index does not freeze casting, pitch, timbre, accent/dialect, DSP, timing, or runtime audio.

Hard boundaries:

- gender/presentation != voice
- body size != pitch/loudness
- age != weak/slow voice
- skin/origin != accent
- disability != weak/childlike/inspirational voice
- artificial body != flat/appliance voice
- animal != Human dialogue sound-button translation
- twins != mandatory matching/opposite voices

## Alias boundary

Stable Author DB aliases remain navigation/data mappings only. `kage1`, `kage2`, `kage3`, `kage4`, and `yubi` do not become in-world spoken forms from being stable profile IDs.

## Era boundary

Era can constrain vocabulary and conventions. It does not automatically generate archaic speech, slang, dialect, accent, pitch, or personality. Exact period language requires separate research and Canon promotion.

## No-flattening rules

- Current != Candidate != Author Reservoir != Open
- exact wording remains Open until screenplay/voice decisions promote it
- exact casting remains Open
- name form does not become relationship score
- voice profile does not become protected-trait evidence
- Future15 coverage does not promote Current21
- runtime does not automatically consume language/voice Reservoirs

## Profile-book value

These two dimensions make the author-facing profile useful for scene writing: the DB can answer not only **what this character is like**, but also **how they approach a name, a pause, a correction, a silence, or a line delivery** without freezing final dialogue.
