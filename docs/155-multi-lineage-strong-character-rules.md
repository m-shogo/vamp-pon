# 155. Multi-Lineage Strong Character Rules

This document defines dual-lineage and triple-lineage characters.

They can exist on both ally and enemy sides.
They are strong, rare, and narratively expensive.

## Core rule

A character with two or three lineages is not strong because they have more elements.
They are strong because multiple memories, families, or vessels are forced to coexist.

Power must come with weight.

## Why multi-lineage exists

Allowed reasons:

- born between two lineages
- adopted into another line
- vessel was repaired with another vessel's part
- memory was split across two families
- spirit pact joined two lights
- black ink fused unrelated memories
- enemy stole and grafted another vessel
- a family tried to preserve someone by combining lights

Forbidden reasons:

- cool because two colors
- random rare power
- stronger version with no cost
- design convenience only

## Two-lineage rule

Dual-lineage characters have one main vessel and one secondary light.

They must show:

- primary lineage
- secondary lineage
- reason for coexistence
- cost or instability
- relation to Yui or another core character
- one hidden blank

## Three-lineage rule

Triple-lineage characters are exceptional.

Use them for:

- late boss
- hidden ally
- unstable rival
- season key character
- old family secret

They must not be common.
A triple-lineage character should feel like a walking contradiction.

## Power cost

Choose at least one cost:

- memory loss gets worse when power is used
- vessel cracks or dims
- one lineage rejects another
- power changes based on stage condition
- strong burst followed by weakness
- black ink is attracted to the character
- relation with another character is damaged

## Ally pattern

A strong ally with two lineages should be useful but incomplete.

Examples:

- Fire + Water: steam guard, healing burn, painful preservation
- Fire + Lightning: flash flame, fast burst, unstable emotion
- Water + Light: moon tide, reveal through tears, gentle support
- Light + Dark: eclipse shield, truth and concealment together
- Lightning + Neutral: machine pulse, scan and chain, functional but lonely

## Enemy pattern

A strong enemy with two or three lineages should feel wrong, not simply cool.

Examples:

- Fire + Dark: ash wraith, burning hidden memory
- Water + Dark: drowned ink mirror, memory kept too long
- Lightning + Dark: broken signal, false recall
- Light + Dark + Fire: eclipse flame boss, truth burns what it reveals
- Fire + Water + Lightning: unstable fused vessel, family experiment or accident

## Design rule

Do not make multi-lineage characters rainbow characters.

Use hierarchy:

- primary vessel shape
- secondary glow accent
- hidden third sign only in motion, attack, or reveal scene

At 1x, the character must still read as one identity.

## Gameplay rule

Two-lineage does not mean two full weapons at start.

Recommended forms:

- mode switch
- conditional upgrade
- delayed second effect
- risk burst
- support plus damage
- enemy phase change

Triple-lineage should usually be boss, late unlock, or story-limited.

## Relation rule

Multi-lineage must create relationship pressure.

Ask:

- which family claims this character?
- which family rejects them?
- who remembers the truth?
- who benefits if the truth stays hidden?
- what did Yui see once, but not understand?

## P1 boundary

Do not implement multi-lineage characters in Prototype 1.

P1 can contain only traces:

- two-color scorch mark
- cracked vessel part
- item with mismatched glow
- unreadable family mark
- rumor text or background prop
