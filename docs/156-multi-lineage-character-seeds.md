# 156. Multi-Lineage Character Seeds

These are heavy seed ideas for strong ally and enemy characters.

They are not immediate implementation targets.
Use them to enrich later arcs and background traces.

## Strong ally seeds

| Seed | Lineages | Vessel | Strength | Heavy reason | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| steam sibling | fire + water | cracked kettle lamp | guard and mist flame | born between fire line and water line | every use cools Yui's flame clue |
| flash torch | fire + lightning | torch with wire | fast burst flame | torch was repaired with a lightning vessel | power burns too quickly |
| moon bottle | water + light | bottle with moon lens | reveal through mist | sealed memory became a guide | cannot reveal without losing one hidden feeling |
| eclipse child | light + dark | covered star lamp | shield and reveal | protected truth by hiding it | truth hurts nearby allies |
| road scanner | lightning + neutral | headlight with old battery | scan and chain | artificial light learned a spirit rhythm | emotion is hard to read |

## Design notes

- Strong allies should still have one readable silhouette.
- Strong enemies may have fractured silhouettes, but must still show attack role.
- Multi-lineage marks should be asymmetric.
- The third lineage should often appear only during attack or reveal.

## Story notes

Good multi-lineage characters should make the player ask:

- who made this vessel?
- who was saved by this fusion?
- who was harmed by it?
- why does Yui remember only one half?
- why does one family deny the relation?

## Use timing

- Season 1: traces only
- Future page bundle: dual-lineage ally or rival
- Rival arc: dual-lineage enemy
- Late season / hidden page: triple-lineage boss or hidden ally
