## Adopted canon

- Black ink shadows are wrong readings, not simple monsters.
- Fighting means loosening the wrong reading so a memory can be read again.
- Yui's lantern is a reading light as well as a fire vessel.
- Four-lineage and five-lineage beings are story events, not normal power upgrades.
- A happy ending keeps some blanks, but makes the remaining meaning gentle enough to carry.
- Important items must have owner, context, wrong reading, gameplay role, reversal, and final or open meaning.

## Priority

When designing future characters or items, use this order:

1. current canon index
2. light lineage rules
3. protagonist relationship web
4. multi-lineage and higher-order rules
5. story street map
6. older idea documents

## Implementation boundary

Do not add multi-character systems, high-order lineage mechanics, or relationship mechanics to P1 from these docs.
Use them as trace placement and future-proofing.
