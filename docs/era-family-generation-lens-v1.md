# 1. 日本の世帯は一種類ではない

Historical / statistical researchで使う分類:

- 単独世帯
- 夫婦のみ
- 夫婦 + 未婚の子
- ひとり親 + 未婚の子
- 三世代世帯
- その他親族 / 非典型household

Research note:

- 日本の公的統計でも、核家族 / 三世代 / 単独 / 高齢者世帯など複数のhousehold typeを分けて扱っている。
- 時代によって同居率・単独世帯・高齢者のみ世帯等の比率は変わる。

Therefore:

> **一つのEra = 一つの家族像**

にしない。

# 2. Generation Lens set

Era sceneを作る時、必要に応じて以下から複数視点を置く。

## Child Lens

見るもの:

- school
- lunch
- playground
- pocket money
- toys / games
- TV / phone / network
- adults' rules that make no sense
- what food feels special

Child should not become exposition device.

## Teen / Young Adult Lens

- fashion
- slang
- school / entrance / job hunting
- dating / friendship
- music / media
- first phone / first wage
- adults' outdated rules
- future anxiety

## Mother Lens

Not automatically:

- housewife
- gentle caretaker
- sacrifice machine

Possible concerns:

- work / household balance
- food / price / childcare
- school / health
- neighborhood relation
- paid / unpaid labor
- own hobbies / career / relationships

## Father Lens

Not automatically:

- breadwinner
- absent salaryman
- strict authority

Possible concerns:

- work / union / company pressure
- household money
- childcare participation
- local community
- job loss / career change
- aging parents

## Aunt / Uncle Lens

Important because they are neither parent nor grandparent.

Possible functions:

- parent generationの常識に少し距離がある
- childへ別のadult modelを見せる
- unmarried / divorced / remarried / living elsewhere等、household diversityを自然に出す
- family内で新しい文化を先に持ち込む
- job / city / migrationの違いを持ち込む
- 酒席 / 趣味 / music / gadget等のEra textureを運ぶ

**伯父叔母を面白い脇役だけにせず、自分の人生を持たせる。**

## Grandmother / Grandfather Lens

Not automatically:

- old-fashioned wisdom
- conservative obstacle
- dead / sick

Possible functions:

- previous Eraとの比較ができる
- food / repair / etiquette / local memory
- social changeを「前より良くなった /悪くなった」両方で見る
- young Characterより新技術を楽しむ人もいてよい

## Older Adult Living Alone Lens

- independence
- shopping / transport access
- loneliness / community
- pride / asking for help
- technology access

## Childless Couple / Single Adult Lens

Family sceneをparents + childrenだけにしない。

- work
- siblings / nephews / nieces
- friends / chosen family
- hobbies
- caregiving of parents
- living alone by choice or circumstance

# 3. Same Era, different problem visibility

同じ社会事件でも世代で見え方を変える。

## Postwar recovery / scarcity Era

### Child

- 「今日のおやつがあるか」
- school lunch
- reused clothes / toys
- adultsが食べ物を残さない理由

### Parent

- food / fuel / housing / work
- ration / informal exchange
- childrenへ不足をどこまで見せるか

### Aunt / Uncle

- new job / city movement
- black market / official distributionへの距離
- familyへ物を持ち帰る役かもしれない

### Grandparent

- prewar / wartime / postwarの生活差を比較できる
- 「前はこうだった」を絶対正解にしない

## Growth / pollution / energy-transition Era

### Child

- new road / new townを単純に楽しいと感じる
- air / water / smellの違和感を大人より先に言うこともある

### Parent

- company / income / home ownership / health
- development benefitを受けながらriskも抱える

### Aunt / Uncle

- urban youth culture / worker / activist / small business等、多様な立場

### Grandparent

- disappearing old route / local land use memory
- 「昔の方が良い」だけにしない

## Early mobile-Internet Era

### Child / Teen

- first e-mail / handle name / bulletin board
- parentsが知らないonline space

### Parent

- Internetを便利だが怖いものとして見る
- school / job / household communicationの変化

### Aunt / Uncle

- familyで最初にPC / phoneへ詳しいadultになれる
- niece / nephewへtechnologyを教える

### Grandparent

- digital divideだけでなく、mail / photo exchangeを楽しむ場合もある

## Present Era

### Child

- school device / short video / group chatが最初からある
- online/offlineを大人ほど分けない

### Parent

- screen time / education / safety / work chat / household cost

### Aunt / Uncle

- childへ「親には言いにくいことを言えるadult」になれる
- unmarried / remote worker / creator / care worker等、多様な生活model

### Grandparent

- smartphone / video call / cashlessを使う人も使わない人もいる
- scam fear / health / mobility / loneliness / grandchildrenとのconnection

## Far Future

Family itself may diversify.

- biological parent
- legal parent
- creator / manufacturer must NOT automatically equal parent
- adopted / chosen family
- Human + Android household
- copied memory branch persons who call each other sibling / not sibling
- one caregiver controlling several avatars
- grandparent with replaced body
- child raised partly by Robot caregiver

Important:

> **Technologyが増えても「家族とは何か」を一つの制度答えへ固定しない。**

# 4. Cross-generation satire

## Child → Adult

Children can expose adult contradiction with simple questions.

Candidate:

> 「みんな“節電”って言うのに、看板はずっと光ってるの？」

> 「知らない人に名前言っちゃだめなのに、学校の名簿には全部書くの？」

> 「大人はスマホ見すぎるなって言うのに、仕事の連絡ずっと見てるね。」

Children are not always morally right; they simply reveal contradictions adults stopped noticing.

## Grandparent → Present young person

Candidate:

> 「そんなに写真があるのに、探してる写真は見つからないのかい。」

> 「便利になったのに、みんな“時間がない”って言うんだねえ。」

Avoid wise-old-person omniscience.

## Young person → Grandparent

Candidate:

> 「昔の決まりだからって、今も守る理由になる？」

> 「それ、“我慢できた”じゃなくて、“断れなかった”じゃない？」

Do not make young person always correct either.

## Aunt / Uncle → Parent

Aunt / Uncle can say what child cannot.

Candidate:

> 「その子のためって言うけど、その子に聞いた？」

Parent can respond with a real responsibility the aunt/uncle does not carry.

This creates useful friction without villainizing family.

# 5. Food is generation evidence

同じEraでも食べ物の意味が違う。

## Child

- treat / school lunch / festival food
- what adults prohibit
- what feels luxurious

## Parent

- price
- nutrition
- time to cook
- leftovers
- household allocation

## Grandparent

- preservation
- seasonality
- old substitute foods
- memories attached to preparation

## Aunt / Uncle

- restaurant / alcohol / new foreign food / hobby cooking
- brings unfamiliar food into family

Dream food scenes can invert these memories:

> same table, different meaning.

Example:

- child sees meat as celebration
- parent sees cost / quantity
- grandparent sees waste
- young adult sees trend / taste

No one interpretation is the official one.

# 6. Family-role satire must not become stereotype

Reject:

- father = work only
- mother = cooking only
- grandma = old wisdom only
- grandpa = stubborn only
- uncle = drunk joke only
- aunt = gossip only
- child = pure truth only

Every recurring family NPC should have at least:

```txt
own work / study / daily role
own pleasure
own irritation
one relationship outside the protagonist
one opinion protagonist disagrees with
one scene where protagonist is not present
```

This prevents them from becoming Character-explanation furniture.

# 8. Scene design formula

For an Era ordinary-life episode:

```txt
Era pressure
×
Age / family role
×
ordinary object or meal
×
relationship tension
=
small scene that teaches the Era without lecture
```

Examples:

```txt
1970s-like energy pressure
× child
× television / room light
× parent says save electricity
→ child notices bright commercial sign outside
```

```txt
Present misinformation
× grandmother
× family group chat
× fake warning image
→ grandchild corrects her, then later shares an unverified clip themselves
```

```txt
Future identity system
× copied adult + child
× family registration screen
× system accepts only one legal parent identity
→ philosophical problem appears as a household problem
```

# 9. World QA

Each major Era should eventually have a cast sample containing:

- at least one child perspective
- at least one young-adult perspective
- at least one parent-age adult perspective
- at least one older-adult perspective
- at least one non-parent adult / aunt-uncle-like perspective
- at least one household structure that is not parents + children

These are **world population / scene design requirements**, not mandatory Core5 family members.

Goal:

> **Eraを一人で代表させない。**

A society feels real when people in the same year disagree about what their year means.
